# On-device performance benchmarks for phone LLMs (as of 2026-09-30)

**TL;DR:** I found no benchmark, as of 2026-09-30, that measures "offline research AI on a phone" end to end. That would mean an offline corpus, retrieval, factual QA judged against frontier models or the internet, confident errors and refusals, and phone speed, memory and energy together. The closest standardized one is **MLPerf Mobile v6.0** (2026-06-09). It runs Llama 1B/3B/8B on real Android and iOS phones and reports token throughput, with MMLU and IFEval accuracy validation. It has no retrieval, no corpus and no comparison with frontier models. The cheapest thing to plug into is the **PocketPal leaderboard**, which runs llama.cpp pp512/tg128 on 386 devices. The closest research artifacts that pair device speed with task quality are MobileAIBench (2024), PalmBench (ICLR 2025) and one RAG benchmark study (AINA 2026), and they are narrow. BOAR would have to add its own layer: a quality eval set plus app-level measurements on the device.

Notes: pp = prompt processing (prefill) tok/s, tg = text generation (decode) tok/s, TTFT = time to first token. The "Real phone?" column shows where measurements run. I checked the dates against primary pages: GitHub releases API, mlcommons.org, primatelabs.com release notes, the arXiv API, and huggingface.co.

## Closest to our need

- **Nothing combines the four axes BOAR needs:** (1) an offline corpus with retrieval, (2) open factual/research QA quality compared with frontier models or internet answers, (3) confident errors and refusal calibration, and (4) real-phone TTFT, tok/s, RAM, energy and thermals over a sustained session. Each benchmark above covers at most two of these.
- **Speed axis, standardized:** MLPerf Mobile v6.0/v6.0.1 LLM test (real phones, Llama 1B/3B/8B, MMLU/IFEval gate), plus `llama-bench` pp512/tg128/`-d 4096` and the PocketPal leaderboard for public comparison. Copy MLPerf Client's TTFT/TPS definitions and its **agentic end-to-end timing** (LLM plus tool time) for our "retrieve + answer" loop.
- **Quality-plus-device pairing, research:** MobileAIBench (quality × quantization plus iPhone cost, including trust and safety) and PalmBench (on-device **hallucination** counts plus power and memory) are the closest method templates for a "confident error" axis.
- **Offline RAG, end to end:** arXiv 2606.11257 (Wikipedia-passage RAG, energy plus GPT-4.1-judged quality, but on a laptop NPU) and MobileRAG (on-phone ANN recall@10, memory and power) together define the retrieval half. The AINA 2026 study shows that small LLM + RAG hits 100% on closed-domain manual QA, which is too easy to settle the ">50% of frontier" claim.
- **Method requirements to adopt:** sustained multi-query runs with thermal logging (2603.23640), prefill and decode energy split by backend (2607.05475), and SLO attainment (% of queries with TTFT < X s, from ConsumerBench). Model sizing at about 3.5 bits per weight or higher (2505.15030).
- **Implication:** BOAR's benchmark has to be our own composite: the eval set (quality against a frontier reference, judged) × the on-device harness (llama-bench-style numbers plus app-level TTFT, peak RSS and energy over N sequential queries). External numbers (MLPerf Mobile, PocketPal) are useful only as hardware calibration.

## Decisions / Options / Tasks

- **Decisions:** Do not claim an existing benchmark validates "offline research AI on a phone"; none does. Use external suites only for the speed and hardware axis.
- **Options:** (a) Run the MLPerf Mobile v6.0.1 LLM test on our target phone as a third-party baseline. (b) Submit our device and model to the PocketPal leaderboard. (c) Port the 2606.11257 RAG benchmark design to a phone.
- **Tasks:** Define BOAR's composite metrics: quality relative to frontier, confident-error rate, TTFT p50/p95 at RAG context, tg tok/s at `-d 4096`, peak RAM, and energy and temperature after 20 queries.
