## Conflicts (decisions in REVIEW-6.md)
1. **Child/baby: 5 rescue breaths first vs compressions first.** SJA, BRC, RCUK (UK, also the existing drowning card) = 5 breaths, then 30:2 (CPR-SJA-CHILD-5, CPR-BRC-CHILD, CPR-RCUK-CHILD-5). healthdirect (Australia/ANZCOR) and the MS Brazil SAMU protocol (AHA 2015 basis) = start with compressions, 30:2, no initial breaths (CPR-HD-CHILD, CPR-MS-PED-START). AHA's own pages could not be fetched (403). Cards follow the UK.
2. **Baby compressions: two fingers vs two-thumb encircling.** Two fingers: SJA, BRC, healthdirect, MS Brazil, Argentina 'Técnica A'. Two thumbs: RCUK 2025 public page, Argentina 'Técnica B' (CPR-RCUK-BABY-THUMBS). Card = two fingers (matches the reviewer's infant-thrusts decision).
3. **AED on a baby.** healthdirect: "Do not use an AED on children under 1 year of age." MS Brazil SAMU protocol: paediatric pads, else adult pads, in a baby. UK lay pages: no infant exclusion. The baby card does not mention an AED.
4. **Adult depth.** 5–6 cm (SJA, MS Brazil, Argentina MS) vs 'about a third of the chest' (healthdirect) vs '5 cm aproximadamente' (Cruz Roja AR). Rate 100–120/min is common to all; BRC/RCUK say '2 per second'.
5. **Lone rescuer, child/baby.** 1 minute of CPR before calling when no speaker phone (SJA, BRC) vs 2 minutes (Argentina MS, CPR-AR-BEBE-ALONE). Adults: call first, on speaker (SJA, RCUK; BRC 'call 999 before you start').
6. **Drinks after a swallowed poison.** NHS/SJA/BRC: nothing to eat or drink; CIATox-ES: no water, milk or other liquid unless a CIATox says so; Cruz Roja AR: 'no beber nada'; poison.org: a small amount of water or milk at once for a burning/caustic product if awake and able to swallow (POIS-PC-MILK). Card = nothing, unless the poison centre or emergency service says so.
7. **Call tier for a swallowed poison.** UK: 999/A&E for any suspected harmful swallow, even with no symptoms (NHS, BRC). AU/US/BR/AR: poison line, emergency number if collapsed, seizure, breathing trouble or won't wake. Card: emergency services or a poison centre now; emergency straight away for the red flags.
8. **Child age bound.** RCUK 'child (aged 1-18)'; healthdirect: child technique only while the chest is too small for two hands; AHA/MedlinePlus: up to puberty (not quoted here).
