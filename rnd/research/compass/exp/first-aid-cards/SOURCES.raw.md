## Conflicts

1. **SCORP-3, cold vs warm.** CDC NIOSH says "Apply ice, but do not submerge…" (†). Brazil (SES-SP alert + federal PCDT 2025) says warm compress, and SES-SP explicitly says "Não colocar gelo ou água fria… pois acentua a dor." A card for Brazil should follow the Brazilian guidance or leave temperature out.
2. **BATT-HONEY.** poison.org recommends 10 mL every 10 min, up to 6 doses, age ≥12 months, within 12 h, en route to the ER. CAPT (UK) says UK doctors prioritise getting to A&E unless the trip is long. NHS WY says [NHS quote withheld]. So the UK omits honey and does not recommend it routinely.
3. **CHOKE-INFANT-2, chest-thrust technique.** NHS and BRC say two fingers, ‘middle of their chest just below the nipple(s)’. SJA says the "two-thumb encircling technique". None use the draft's [quote withheld] wording.
4. **CHOKE-CHILD order.** NHS: [NHS quote withheld] BRC and SJA give a fixed sequence (back blows, then thrusts).
