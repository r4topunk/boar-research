## Compass review (2026-10-03)
- **CI:** with n = 5 and two single-item categories, the stratified CI is degenerate. The headline now uses the plain CI, **0.268–0.507**, which contains 0.50 while the point estimate is 0.390. Read it as *bar not shown*. It is not a claim that the bar fails. Either way, the V-exact slice never changes the main verdict (plan §3).
- **≥ Haiku-no-web per item:** met on 1/5 (vx-002, 0.53 vs 0.47). That margin is within noise: one item, two seeds, against a single Haiku run.
- **Venue city naming:** under the V-para P5 definition (≥ 3 real venues **and** the GPS city named in the answer), V-exact passes **5/6**. vx-005 lists real Tokyo venues ("Vegan places near you") but never says Tokyo.
- **What the losses are (4B answers, both seeds, read by Compass; his questions are public):**
  - **Gríma (0.20): a confident false correction.** "Gríma Wormtongue does not kill Saruman…" Both seeds invent a different killer (Aragorn's Rangers at the Black Gate; Rohan soldiers at the Fords of Isen). In the book, Gríma does kill Saruman (the Scouring of the Shire). Only an uncited warning is attached.
  - **Japan (0.38):** routed as `task:chat`. Retrieval comes from the crypto kit, so both seeds say Japan "has established regulatory frameworks for stablecoins". There is no general encyclopedic source in this kit (boar-crypto + wikivoyage-en).
  - **Mozi (0.54):** `grounding:no-good-source`, so the answer is an uncited model answer with the "not from an offline source" preface.
  - **Paris–Rome (0.29):** "~1,200 km one way… not feasible… not recommended or realistic". It treats the question as a feasibility judgement instead of answering it. The distance is also on the low side.
