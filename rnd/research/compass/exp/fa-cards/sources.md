|---|---|---|---|
| CO | NHS: "go outside"; "get medical advice as soon as possible – do not go back into the affected building until you have got advice". CDC/NIOSH †: "Get to fresh air and seek medical help immediately" | NHS: "Do not drive to A&E." CDC †: "Do not drive a motor vehicle–get someone else to drive you" | nhs.uk/conditions/carbon-monoxide-poisoning/ ; cdc.gov/niosh/carbon-monoxide/about/index.html |
| Burns | NHS: "Hold the burn or scald under cool running water for 20 minutes. Do this as soon as possible, within 3 hours of it happening." | NHS: "do not put any creams, oils or butter on burns or scalds"; "do not burst any blisters". SJA: "Do not use ice, creams or gels." | nhs.uk/conditions/burns-and-scalds/ ; sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/burns-and-scalds/ |
| Heatstroke | NHS: "Wrap the person in a cool, wet sheet, or fan them or sponge them down with cold water while you wait for help to arrive." CDC/NIOSH †: "Call 911 for emergency medical care." | MedlinePlus: "Do not give the person anything by mouth (not even salted drinks) if the person is vomiting or unconscious." | nhs.uk/conditions/heat-exhaustion-heatstroke/ ; cdc.gov/niosh/heat-stress/about/illnesses.html ; medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000056.htm |
| Hypothermia | NHS: "move the person indoors or somewhere sheltered as quickly as possible"; "remove any wet clothing, wrap them in a blanket, sleeping bag or dry towel, making sure their head is covered" | NHS: "do not use a hot bath, hot water bottle or heat lamp to warm them up"; "do not rub their arms, legs, feet or hands"; "do not give them alcohol to drink". MedlinePlus: "removed from the cold environment with as little exertion as possible" | nhs.uk/conditions/hypothermia/ (review overdue) ; medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000038.htm |
| Frostbite | WMS 2024: "Rapidly rewarm in water heated and maintained between 37° and 39°C (98.6° to 102.2°F) until area becomes soft and pliable to the touch (approximately 30 minutes)." NHS: "place the affected body part in warm (not hot) water" | NHS: "do not rub the affected area"; "do not use things like a fire, radiator, hot water bottle or heat lamp". WMS: "If environmental conditions are such that thawed tissue could refreeze, it is safer to keep the affected part frozen…" | wms.org/magazine/magazine/1494/Frostbite-Summary-2024/default.aspx ; nhs.uk/conditions/frostbite/ |
| Choking | SJA: "give five sharp back blows" / "give five abdominal thrusts" / "Repeat five back blows and five abdominal thrusts until help arrives". MedlinePlus: "repeated cycles of 5 back blows followed by 5 abdominal thrusts" | MedlinePlus: "Do not interfere if the person is coughing forcefully…" | sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/choking/ ; medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000049.htm (NHS adult page 404; US Red Cross 410) |
| Bleeding | SJA: "Apply direct firm pressure to the wound…". MedlinePlus: "Put pressure directly on an external wound with a sterile bandage, clean cloth, or even a piece of clothing." | MedlinePlus: "Do not remove a dressing if it becomes soaked with blood. Instead, add a new one on top." (SJA: remove and reapply: conflict) | sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/severe-bleeding/ ; medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000045.htm |
| Heart attack | NHS: "Take aspirin (300mg) if you have any – do not give aspirin to someone who has an aspirin allergy." SJA: "ask them to chew it slowly" | NHS: "Do not drive yourself to A&E." SJA: not if under 16 | nhs.uk/conditions/heart-attack/ ; sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/heart-attack/ |
| Stroke | NHS: "FAST… face, arms, speech and time to call 999"; "Call 999 now if… even if they've now stopped". CDC †: "Note the time when any symptoms first appear." | CDC †: "Do not drive to the hospital or let someone else drive you." | nhs.uk/conditions/stroke/symptoms/ ; cdc.gov/stroke/signs-symptoms/index.html |
| Seizure | NHS: "cushion their head if they're on the ground"; "turn them on to their side after their convulsions stop"; 999 if "the seizure lasts more than 5 minutes, if you do not know how long their seizures usually last" | NHS: "Do not put anything in their mouth, including your fingers." CDC †: "Don't hold the person down or stop their movements." | nhs.uk/symptoms/what-to-do-if-someone-has-a-seizure-fit/ ; cdc.gov/epilepsy/first-aid-for-seizures/index.html |
| Anaphylaxis | NHS: "Use an adrenaline auto-injector (such as an EpiPen) if you have one"; "Call 999… say that you think you're having an anaphylactic reaction."; "Lie down – you can raise your legs…"; "If your symptoms have not improved after 5 minutes, use a 2nd adrenaline auto-injector." | NHS: "Do not stand or walk at any time, even if you feel better." | nhs.uk/conditions/anaphylaxis/ (review overdue) |
| Button battery | CAPT: "Take them straight to the A&E department at your local hospital or dial 999 for an ambulance." | CAPT: "Do not let your child eat or drink."; "Do not make them sick." (NCPC honey: ≥ 12 months, never delay; UK doesn't push it) | capt.org.uk/button-batteries-in-an-emergency/ ; poison.org/battery |
| Chemical in eye | NHS: "run lots of water over your eyeball for at least 20 minutes"; "keep rinsing your eye with water while waiting for medical help, and take the container with you" | NHS: "do not touch or rub your eye until it's better" | nhs.uk/conditions/eye-injuries/ |
| Electric shock | SJA: "Turn off the source of electricity…". MedlinePlus: "If you can do so safely, turn off the electrical current." | SJA: "Do not touch the casualty if they're still in contact with the electrical source". MedlinePlus: "Do not get within 20 feet (6 meters)… power lines" | sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/electrocution/ ; medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000053.htm (NHS page 404) |
| Jellyfish | NHS: "rinse the affected area with seawater (not fresh water)"; "soak the area in very warm water (as hot as can be tolerated) for at least 30 minutes". healthdirect: tropical: "remove any tentacles… pour vinegar on the sting site and go to hospital" | NHS: "do not pee on the sting"; "do not apply ice or a cold pack". healthdirect: bluebottle: "Do not put vinegar on the sting." | nhs.uk/conditions/jellyfish-and-other-sea-creature-stings/ ; healthdirect.gov.au/jellyfish-stings |
| Nosebleed | NHS: "sit down and lean forward, with your head tilted forward"; "pinch your nose just above your nostrils for 10 to 15 minutes" | SJA: "Do not tell them to lean their head back…" | nhs.uk/conditions/nosebleed/ ; sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/nosebleed/ |
| Earthquake | Ready.gov: "Drop where you are onto hands and knees." "Cover your head and neck…" "Hold until the shaking stops." | Ready.gov: "If you are inside, stay and do not run outside; avoid doorways." | ready.gov/earthquakes |
