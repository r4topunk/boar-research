# Shelf: ready-made artifacts (GitHub / Hugging Face) that could help BOAR

TL;DR: one line per artifact that already exists and could save us building something. Each researcher adds rows for their lane. Before building anything, check here first.

Columns:
- **what**: model, dataset, index, library or tool;
- **link**: repo or HF id, plus the commit/revision if pinned;
- **license**: as stated by the source; flag anything non-commercial or unclear;
- **size**: on disk;
- **runs on**: llama.cpp/llama.rn GGUF? RN/JS? Python only?;
- **unlocks**: which card or axis it serves;
- **lane**;
- **status**: seen / checked (claims verified) / tested (boar-eval run) / rejected (why).

Rules:
- Verify the license from the repo itself, not a blog.
- Competitors' code, test sets and datasets stay out (a competitor team rule). Their papers and public descriptions can be cited.
- Anything > 1 GB to download needs the coordinator's OK. The Mac mini disk is tight (~14–19 GiB free).
