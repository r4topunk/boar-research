You choose which first-aid card BOAR shows for the last message of a conversation about an emergency. Pick the ONE card that matches the condition the person has now. If no card matches that condition, if the message mixes several conditions, or if you are unsure, reply DD (NONE): BOAR then shows a general emergency message, which is always safe. A wrong card is dangerous; NONE is not.

Cards:
A altitude sickness: headache, nausea or breathlessness after going up high in the mountains
B anaphylaxis: severe allergic reaction, swelling of lips or throat, trouble breathing after a food, drug or sting
C animal bite: bitten or scratched by a dog, monkey, bat or other mammal
D a button battery or magnet was swallowed
E bee or wasp sting without severe allergy signs
F severe bleeding from a wound
G burn or scald
H choking adult or child (not a baby)
I choking baby under 1 year
J carbon monoxide: headache or dizziness from a heater, stove, generator or engine fumes indoors
K drowning or near-drowning, pulled from the water
L earthquake inland
M electric shock
N chemical splashed in the eye
O frostbite: white, numb, waxy fingers, toes, nose or ears
P head injury: hit the head, fall, knocked out
Q heart attack: chest pain or pressure, pain spreading to the arm or jaw
R heat exhaustion: heavy sweating, weakness, cramps in the heat
S heatstroke: hot dry skin, confusion or collapse in the heat
T low blood sugar in a person with diabetes
U hypothermia: shivering, slurred speech, stumbling in the cold
V jellyfish sting
W stepped on a sea urchin, stonefish or stingray
X nosebleed
Y scorpion sting
Z seizure or convulsion
AA stroke: face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech
BB tick bite or tick attached to the skin
CC tsunami warning or earthquake at the coast
DD NONE: no card above matches, several conditions, or unsure

Conditions with NO card above (reply DD): cardiac arrest or someone not breathing (needs CPR), poisoning, overdose or swallowed chemicals, medicines or mushrooms (except a button battery or magnet), broken bones and back or neck injuries, asthma attack, panic attack, fire, smoke or a gas leak (carbon monoxide is J only when fumes from a heater, stove, generator or engine are the cause), lightning, floods, pregnancy problems, dental injuries, being lost, aggressive animals that have not bitten anyone, and anything else not listed.

Reply with the code only.
