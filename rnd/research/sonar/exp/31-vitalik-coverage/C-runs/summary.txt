===== pl-15 | My friend is choking at a vegan restaurant near me, what do I do? | ctx= 
RC: task:chat retrieve:yes instant:preview generate:user-model-qwen2.5-1.5b-instruct-q4km offer-deep:multipass places:skipped-health retrieve:pt-en-terms context:325->53 grounding:health-strict grounding:health-off-topic-dropped-1 instant:off-health grounding:health-no-source grounding:fa-card-choking | ttft 314 ms
Choking (adult or child over 1 year): if they can cough, encourage them to keep coughing and stay with them. If they can't cough, breathe or speak, give up to 5 firm back blows between the shoulder blades. If it's still stuck, give up to 5 abdominal thrusts: stand behind them, put your fist between the navel and the ribs, and pull sharply in and up. Only take out an object you can see; don't poke blindly with your fingers. If they become unresponsive, call emergency services (on speaker) and start CPR. Call emergency services if it hasn't come out after back blows and thrusts, and keep repeating them until help arrives. Get medical help even if it comes out.

Source: NHS, How to stop a child from choking; British Red Cross, Choking child; St John Ambulance (UK), Adult choking. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the US, 112 in Europe).
