Previews draw the exact vector from the PR: path scaled 0.5, moved 24 / 26.75 dp, cropped to the 72 dp visible area. The ear tips sit 33.4 dp from centre, on the edge of the 33 dp safe zone and inside every mask. The themed tint is an example; the phone picks it from the wallpaper.

The mark · 120-unit grid

What is in it

  • EarsTwo triangles, offset 6 units from the forehead edge.
  • ForeheadHexagon, the largest plate; it carries the visual weight.
  • SnoutFlat hexagon with two nostril slots cut through, so the background shows.
  • TusksShards that follow the snout's lower edges at the same 6-unit gutter, tips up and out.
  • GutterEvery gap is negative space of one width. No outlines, no gradients, one colour.
