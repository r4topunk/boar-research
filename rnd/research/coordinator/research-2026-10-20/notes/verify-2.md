# verify-2 — adversarial verification of 10 claims (2026-10-20)

TL;DR: 5 confirmed, 5 corrected, 0 refuted. Main corrections: R-Tuning MMLU numbers are AP scores, not accuracy; FaithEval data licence is unstated (code Apache-2.0), not NC; Intellectual Humility's abstract says open-ended QA was limited (BOAR is open-ended); the judge-noise paper is internally inconsistent on "15 trials" (Table 6: 15 trials = 90 %, >50 for 95 % on hard items); the calibration-correction paper corrects BINARY verdicts (sensitivity/specificity), so BOAR's 1-5 scores need a binarisation; the NPU study is now fully identified (4 Snapdragon phones, 1-7.6B models). No claim has evidence at <= 2B except Granite-3.3-2B (RL abstention), Llama-3.2-1B/Qwen2.5-1.5B (speed study), Qwen3-1.7B and Gemma 3 1B (speed cards).
