## Limits
- **Hedge flag:** the judge sees the warning line in warned texts (the WARNCAL hedge flag). This is not corrected, so the Opus correct rates for warned answers are, if anything, low.
- **Small samples:** the S and H-nh samples are small (16 and 11). P(correct | unwarned) and P(CE | unwarned) lean on the Haiku census through the difference estimator.
- **One judge pass:** one Opus pass (two orders), one seed, MacBook texts, dev sets. ST-cand non-health answers are not covered.
