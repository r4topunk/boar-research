## TL;DR
- **Verdict: KILL (frozen rule).** No rule in the family reaches precision >= 0.55 with recall >= 0.60 on MAIN 97 under both label families. The family has 8478 x 386 combinations with at most 3 thresholds.
  - The best rule, chosen on MAIN itself (in-sample), reaches **0.547 / 0.743 under Haiku** and **0.526 / 0.725 under Opus-patched labels**.
  - Even the Haiku-only oracle stops at 0.547.
- **The rule fitted on the 180 self-tests does not transfer.** On FIT (180 self-tests) the selected rule raises precision 0.382 → **0.454** (recall 0.617 → 0.636).
  - On MAIN, the same rule moves precision 0.407 → **0.383** (paired Δ CI −0.086…+0.034) and recall 0.786 → 0.729, under both Haiku and Opus labels.
- **The targeted bucket barely exists on MAIN.** U:kept-weak, the unwarned weak-source answers with CE 0.46, is 2 rows on MAIN. The 0.46 figure comes from the self-tests.
  - Only 15 of MAIN's 70 CEs are unwarned, so adding the line to more answers can recover little.
- **The precision ceiling sits inside the warned answers.** Inside each released rule the CE rate is flat, and no feature separates the cases:
  - The 56 W:all-citations-removed rows: CE 0.55 with max cosine <= 0.70 vs 0.33 above it.
  - The 42 W:uncited-with-sources rows: CE 0.52 vs 0.47.
  - The 37 W:no-source rows: CE 0.19, all with 0 kept sources. Dropping this bucket costs 7 of 70 CEs of recall.
- **Per 100 MAIN rows under refuse-by-default (Haiku):**
  - Before: correct-shown 16.5, CE-shown 4.5.
  - After the selected rule: correct-shown 16.5, CE-shown **5.8** (Δ CI −2.1…+4.8). The rule shows more confident errors and no extra correct answers.
  - Opus-patched gives the same picture: correct-shown 17.5 → 17.5, CE-shown 4.5 → 5.5.

## Rule selected on FIT (in words)
- **Stop warning** a released answer (all-citations-removed or uncited-with-sources) when its best kept chunk has cosine >= 0.70 and the body is >= 150 characters.
- **Start warning** an unwarned answer (kept-weak or grounded) when its best kept cosine is <= 0.70.

## Patch sketch (not applied; for the record only, since the verdict is KILL)
This is where the selected rule would go in src/routing/answer.ts (lp-screens tree be-0cfd4a77-cut7-litepacks), at the `release(rule)` call sites and in `finish()`. Inputs:
- `maxCos` = the max `similarity` over the kept sources.
- `bodyLen` = the length of the answer body.

The logic:
- **Skip** `release()` when the rule is all-citations-removed or uncited-with-sources AND maxCos >= 0.70 AND bodyLen >= 150.
- **Call** `release("weak-cosine")` when the answer has kept sources AND none was released AND maxCos <= 0.70.

Not recommended: it loses on MAIN.

## Reading

  - Inside each released rule, CE and partial answers have the same retrieval footprint (kept count, cosine, on-subject count, citation count, length).
  - Self-test fits pick up pack-specific cosine structure that MAIN, with kit plus simplewiki, does not share.
- **Claim v5 and NOTE-2 keep the WARNCAL/WARN-OPUS sentences.** "About 2 in 5 warned answers are confidently wrong; the line carries about two thirds of confident errors." Don't promise a tuned line.
