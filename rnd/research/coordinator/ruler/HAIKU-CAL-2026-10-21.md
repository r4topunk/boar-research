## TL;DR
- **Haiku is a good CE detector and a poor "correct" detector.** Listed pool: kappa on CE **0.75**, on "correct >= 4" **0.49**, 3-class 0.54. Disagreements are one-directional: CE Haiku-only 39 vs Opus-only 11; correct Opus-only **98** vs Haiku-only 10.
- **Warned answers are where Haiku fails most:** kappa(correct) **0.22** on warned vs 0.65 on unwarned; Opus counts 75 correct where Haiku counts 15 (mean corr shift +0.41 vs +0.17). This is the WARNCAL "hedge penalty" seen from the judge side: Haiku scores the hedged answer down, Opus much less.
- **Calibration curve (Haiku corr bin -> Opus):** 1-1.5 -> P(Opus CE) 0.72, P(correct) 0.00; 2-2.5 -> 0.06 / 0.09; **3-3.5 -> 0.00 / 0.49**; 4-5 -> 0.00 / 0.89. So a Haiku CE is an Opus CE about 3 times in 4, and half of the Haiku 3-3.5 "partials" are Opus-correct (warned: 0.65; unwarned: 0.36).
- **U shift is small and nearly uniform.** dU (Opus - Haiku, ratio of sums) listed +0.022 (0.011-0.033); by tier 1.7B +0.013, Gemma E2B +0.021, 4B +0.051 (listed) / +0.014 (all), 1.5B +0.038, deep35 +0.069.
- **Differential shift between tiers (paired items, same refs):** 4B-1.7B +0.010 (-0.003...+0.022), Gemma-1.7B +0.015 (-0.000...+0.031), 4B-Gemma -0.006, 1.7B-1.5B +0.017, 4B-1.5B +0.016, **deep35-4B +0.026 (+0.004...+0.049)**. The kill "every pair below 0.01" does **not** fire: Haiku compresses gaps by 0.01-0.03 U, always in the direction of shrinking the bigger model's lead.
- **Implied correction for the Haiku-only show-anyway numbers** (map = 1.7B listed cells, Haiku bin x warned, applied to SHOWANYWAY's 492 LP reveals): correct:CE **0.71** (row-bootstrap 0.54-0.94; map error not included) vs Haiku 0.19; per 100 reveals ~22 correct / 31 CE (Haiku 7 / 35). MAIN 0.62, ST-SE 0.52, ST-cand 1.53; non-health 0.55, health 3.0. Consistent with WARN-OPUS's 0.54 (0.32-0.83): **the liability verdict holds under the calibrated map too (ratio < 1 for non-health).**

## Implied correction for Haiku-only numbers
- **Counts:** replace a Haiku count with sum over (Haiku bin x warned) cells of P(Opus label | cell). 1.7B cells (listed): CE 1-1.5 w 0.83 / u 0.71; correct 2-2.5 w 0.19 / u 0.00; 3-3.5 w 0.72 / u 0.24; 4-5 w 0.71 (n 7) / u 0.92. Pooled-tier cells in JSON correction_map_listed.
  - Rule of thumb: Opus CE ~ 0.8 x Haiku CE; Opus correct ~ 2 x Haiku correct overall, ~ 5 x on warned answers.
- **U deltas:** Haiku shifts all tiers up by +0.01 to +0.05 U, and shrinks larger-model leads by +0.010 (4B-1.7B), +0.015 (Gemma-1.7B), +0.026 (deep35-4B). A Haiku-only verdict whose margin to its bar is inside 0.01-0.03 U in the "bigger model loses" direction is suspect; correct-count clauses are suspect at any margin (factor 2-5).

## Reading
- The two conflicting Opus show-anyway numbers are not a judge disagreement: WARN-OPUS (0.54) and CV5-PER100 (1.7) measure different populations. The map puts MAIN reveals at about 0.6 and the unwarned no-kept-source bucket above 1; CV5-PER100's MAIN K0 hidden rows contain both. REVEAL-CENSUS (Task B) measures MAIN directly.
- Registry sweep (CLOSED R79-R98, Haiku-only verdicts): not done here. Candidates by this map = any correct-count clause and any U-delta margin under 0.03 against the larger tier.

## Limits
- Listed sources are not a random sample: WARN-OPUS oversamples health and warned rows; CE-OPUS targets CE rows. Cells are reported per source in the JSON.
- One Opus pass per unit in most cases; Opus test-retest noise is not separated from family shift.
- The bootstrap CI of the implied ratio resamples rows only, not the map cells.
