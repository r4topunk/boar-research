## TL;DR
- **Kill criterion (literal): NOT KILLED, by a hair.** Precision of the warning for CE = **0.39 (CI 0.32-0.46)**: the point is below 0.40, the CI-high is not. Recall = **0.67 (0.59-0.75)**.
- **The hypothesis statement is not fully supported.** It needed a ratio >= 2x and recall >= 60 %. Recall passes. The ratio P(CE|warned) / P(CE|unwarned) = **1.91 (1.41-2.66)**, just under 2.
- **The warning is a much stronger "not correct" signal than a "wrong" signal.** P(correct | warned) = **0.05** (CI 0.03-0.08) vs P(correct | unwarned) = **0.32** (0.24-0.38). Only 15 % of all judge-correct answers (20 of 134) sit behind the line. The rest of the warned answers are mostly partial (corr 2-3.5).
- **It does not hold on the pack-matched self-tests.** On ST-cand 80, precision is 0.22 (0.12-0.32) and recall 0.47 (0.29-0.66). With a matching pack, the confident errors move into cited, unwarned answers.
- **A second sample (MINI) replicates it:** precision 0.39, recall 0.66, ratio 1.67.

## Sensitivities
- **Opus vs Haiku labels (CE-OPUS, 43 non-decline answers selected on Haiku CE, direction only).**
  - Precision: Haiku 0.80, Opus 0.60. Recall: 0.77 vs 0.78.
  - Haiku over-calls CE, so the precision above is, if anything, high under Opus. Recall looks stable.
- **The judge sees the warning line (hedge effect).** The judged text includes "From the model's memory, may be wrong", and one judge rationale calls such an answer "a self-described unverified sketch".
  - We found 34 prefix-matched pairs (17 items) where the same body appears warned in one arm and unwarned in another. Mean corr (warned − unwarned) = **−0.27**; CE 23 vs 17.
  - The line probably costs a fraction of a point in correctness. That inflates precision and depresses P(correct | warned).
  - The pairs are not byte-identical and not independent, so this is a flag, not a correction.
- **Unexplained gap (UNKNOWN):** LITEDECLINE OFF5 on the mini (1.7B + X2b) scored released rule-4 answers at corr >= 4 on 30 of 67. Here, warned uncited-with-sources answers reach corr >= 4 on 5 of 128. The set, kit and line differ, and the hedge effect may explain part of the gap.

## Caveats
- **Post-hoc:** the criterion was frozen before counting, but the data existed and its shape was partly known (CMP30OFF, GATE v2).
- **Judge:** a single Haiku 5.5-low judge (it compresses gaps and over-calls CE vs Opus).
- **Sample:** 1 seed per source. K1/K3 share K0 judgments on identical texts; the cluster bootstrap handles that.
- **Sets:** dev sets only, and the self-tests are pack-written.
- **Proxy:** desktop proxy of the engine, not the Kotlin app.
