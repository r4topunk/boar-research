PeerDAS (EIP-7594) is a networking protocol that enables nodes to perform data availability sampling, ensuring blob data is accessible while downloading only a small subset [1]. Nodes verify availability by downloading just a few random pieces of the data, relying on cryptographic mechanisms to confirm the rest is present without needing the full dataset [3]. This approach allows the network to scale blob throughput without increasing individual node bandwidth or storage requirements [2].
