PeerDAS (EIP-7594) is a networking protocol that enables nodes to perform data availability sampling, ensuring blob data is accessible while downloading only a small subset [1]. It achieves this by dividing blobs into pieces and using cryptographic mechanisms for uniform random distribution, allowing each node to verify availability by checking just a few pieces rather than the entire dataset [3]. This approach scales blob throughput without increasing bandwidth or storage requirements for individual nodes [2].
