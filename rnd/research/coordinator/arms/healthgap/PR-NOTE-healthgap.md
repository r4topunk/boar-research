## Commit message
routing: route dose, poisoning, carbon-monoxide-reading and survival-time questions to the health path

The learned emergency detector was trained on emergency situations, so it scores the planning or information form of four themes near zero: a medicine dose ("How many mg of ibuprofen for a 20 kg child?", "Gave her Calpol at 9 pm, what time can she have more?"), a poisoning ("Is 5 g of nutmeg toxic?"), a carbon monoxide reading ("CO reading 200 ppm in the house") and a survival time ("How long can a 2 year old go without water?"). The health lexicon, the condition-card triggers, the CO-hazard rules and the calculators miss them too, so they reached the model, which answered doses and toxicity from memory. This change adds src/routing/healthGap.ts, a lexical predicate with four classes. A hit counts as a detector fire, so small talk, calculators, FACTS and places are skipped and the health route runs. The receipt keeps the detector's own detector:p and detector:fire and adds healthgap:<class> (plus healthgap:event and healthgap:prev-turn). A dose question never gets a dose from the model: if a kept source's title names the medicine, the answer quotes that source's own sentences that state an amount, cited, followed by the label, child and overdose lines; otherwise it gets a dose line that gives no dose. A survival-time question whose kept source's title names the condition (hypothermia, cold water, dehydration, desert, starvation) is answered by the model from that source under the health grounding; with no such source it gets the existing no-source line. With no source, a specific condition card still wins (a swallowed button battery gets the battery card); otherwise a poisoning gets a new poisoning card and a CO reading the CO card. A poisoning event (a deadly mushroom named, or something swallowed or breathed in) gets its card before any excerpt. A short follow-up ("when can she have more?", "and for a 30 kg kid?", "what about 2 g?") is read together with the previous user turn when that turn has a class of its own. Off by default; on with deps.healthgap = true.

## Known interactions and limits
- PR-1's NUMGUARD runs before this hook. A gap turn NUMGUARD guards is re-fired here, so no calculator runs (code numguard:no-calculator); the arithmetic shortcut still runs on fired turns as before, behind its own health/danger guard.
- Three probes the detector already fired get a better text: two dose questions get the dose line, a lamp-oil ingestion gets the poisoning card. Three follow-up turns of a poisoning conversation now get the poisoning card instead of the generic line.
- Not covered: mixed cleaning-product fumes, a Spanish generator-in-tent question, PT/ES vitamin doses and PT/ES survival questions (they keep the cut 7 path). Not covered by design: red-flag unit conversions and disease-knowledge lookups.
- Accepted false positives: "mg of caffeine in 2 espressos", "Medicine Hat to Calgary", "curso de medicina" and a CO-alarm battery question get a dose line or the CO card; "how long can my dog go without food" gets the survival line.
- Texts: the EN dose line and EN poisoning card passed an independent AI review; the PT/ES texts are reviewer-revised translations. None is clinically reviewed.

## Rule for the native port (health path)
1. Run the four-class predicate on the current user turn before the detector verdict is used; if it has no class and the turn is ≤ 12 words, join the previous user turn when every word is a follow-up word, or when the turn adds a patient or an amount and the previous turn has a class of its own.
2. A hit counts as a detector fire everywhere; keep the detector's p and fire in the receipt and add healthgap:<class>. Precedence: poison-event > co > dose > poison-info > survival; a specific condition card always wins over the poisoning card.
3. Dose: never a model. A kept source whose title names the medicine → its own amount sentences, verbatim and cited, then the label/child/overdose lines; else the dose line. Survival: a kept source titled with the condition → the model answers from it under the health grounding; else the no-source line.
4. Poisoning: no source → the poisoning card; an event → the card before any excerpt. CO → the CO card. The PT texts name the Disque-Intoxicação (0800 722 6001) and SAMU 192 / Bombeiros 193; the ES texts name a centro de toxicología and the local emergency number (911 / 112).
5. Parity contract: the goldens in healthGap.test.ts (64 probes, 240 public questions, 35 examples, the multi-turn cases); any class change fails the build.

## Off switch
Leave deps.healthgap unset (or false): behaviour is exactly the current path (route replay: off = plain cut 7 on 355/355 items; generation controls byte-identical).
