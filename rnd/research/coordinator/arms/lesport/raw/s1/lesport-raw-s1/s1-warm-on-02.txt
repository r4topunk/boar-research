PeerDAS (EIP-7594) is a networking protocol that enables data availability sampling, allowing nodes to verify blob data availability by downloading only a small subset of the data [1]. It achieves this by dividing blobs into pieces and using cryptographic mechanisms to ensure uniform random distribution, so each node checks only a few pieces to satisfy a local availability check [3]. This approach scales blob throughput without increasing bandwidth or storage requirements for individual nodes [2]. [end of text]
