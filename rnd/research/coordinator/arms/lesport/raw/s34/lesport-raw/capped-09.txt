Before EIP-7251, you would have needed 312 validators (using 32 ETH each) plus one additional validator for the remaining 16 ETH, totaling 313 validators. After EIP-7251, the minimum validator threshold remains 32 ETH, so you can still operate 312 full validators, but you can consolidate the remaining balance into a single validator with a higher effective balance rather than needing a separate partial validator.
