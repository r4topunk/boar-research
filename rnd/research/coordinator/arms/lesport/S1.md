What it means:
- Cold on this mini is a non-event: item-1 decode 15.67 vs 15.94 warm (-2 %), same text 10/10 as S0; the M4 internal SSD feeds the 1.20 GiB bank fast enough that decode is compute-bound, not I/O-bound. So S0's 16.2 tok/s was not a page-cache artefact on this host; a phone (UFS, 3–4x slower random reads, fewer big cores) is still unmeasured and the 3–7 tok/s guess stands as an estimate only.
- Under memory pressure (page cache starved) decode drops 16.0 -> 13.9 (-13 %) with prefetch off and 15.5 (-3 %) with prefetch on; lookahead prefetch is lossless (10/10) and worth +8 % warm, +12 % under pressure. Prefill stays ~16–18 s (compute) everywhere.
- Versus bmoe K0 (8.69 tok/s, 10.25 GiB footprint; arms/deepspeed/DEEPSPEED.md): the fork LES is 1.6–2.0x faster at 2.8 GiB RSS even under pressure, but the metrics differ (RSS vs mapped-page footprint) and the host is a desktop M4; the Android port blockers from S0 (stripped file Metal-only, bank cap 1.20 GiB) are unchanged.
