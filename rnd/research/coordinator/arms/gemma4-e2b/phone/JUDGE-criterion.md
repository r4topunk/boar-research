## Estimator (unchanged from DEVICE-X6)
arms/device-x6/tools/agg_device.py functions (sha256 b065bfd39ee41e5f...): per item, mean over the two orders of the mean of correctness/completeness/usefulness for BOAR (s) and the reference (r); raw = sum s / sum r; above_min = sum (s-1) / sum (r-1). Paired difference A - B = raw(A) - raw(B) on the common ids, 95 % CI = 2.5/97.5 percentiles of 2000 item-bootstrap resamples (seed 11). Two-seed desktop references: per-item s and r averaged over s42 and s43 before the ratio. Items judged in only one order are dropped and named.

## Reading rules (frozen)
1. P_G - P_4 (n 57; primary): CI low > 0 -> "Gemma on the phone better than the 4B on the phone"; CI high < 0 -> "4B better"; else "not distinguishable on the 57 (Haiku)". Same rule for the context rows.
2. Parity P_G vs D_G (n 57; desktop = two-seed mean): "phone = desktop within noise" iff |delta| <= 0.03 AND the CI inside [-0.05, +0.05] (the DEVICE-X6 / A15 rule). Two-seed noise band = the paired D_G s43 - s42 delta and its CI on the same ids; the report states whether |P_G - D_G| lies inside that band. Because the configurations differ (kit, engine arm, runtime), a failed parity reads "phone config != desktop config", not "device effect". Config-matched device parity for this phone is reported alongside: P_4 vs desktop 4B cut 7 (same rule).
3. P_G vs 1.7B: P_G - D_17 (n 57, two-seed mean; cross config) and P_G - P_17 (cmp30-q17, n 26, same phone and config, different session): rule 1 wording. n 26 is reported as a subset, not as the 57-id number.
4. One phone run per arm (unseeded phone sampling); per the seed-noise rule a one-run delta < 0.05 is not a robust ordering, and the report says so beside such numbers.
