## What the patch does
Patch: `patches/H2-HEALTH-TOPIC-FALLBACK-never-let-health-strict-run-with-an-empty-or-symptom-only-topic.patch`, sha256 `b0aae5777d4e00a0d6e2dab6bd60044406992b44459f2df15f8edffc0e7ee1f8` (the file also carries the brand-new test file), base `kiln-local/be-0cfd4a77-cut7-litepacks` (not edited), clone `kiln-local/rs-H2` (`cp -c -R`). `diff -ruN base/src rs-H2/src`; `patch -p1` on a fresh clone reproduces rs-H2/src (checked).
Files (sha256 prefix in rs-H2): answer.ts 680e1095b883 (base 0d1ac07d), context.ts e293836040ba (3 existing consts + COMPOUNDS gained `export`, no behaviour change), new `src/routing/healthTopicFallback.ts` a51ba593ecd6, new test `healthTopicFallback.test.ts` 05ad5ffa40b2.
- `healthTopicFallback(query, english, reasonCodes, userTurns)` replaces `healthTopicTerms(req.query, english)` at all five call sites (answer.ts health-strict filter + best-restored pool; head-pass health filter; dense-append health filter; the two `excerptRules` sites, for consistency). `onHealthTopic` and the health-best-restored logic are untouched.
- t = healthTopicTerms(...); (a) the card matcher (`conversationCondition`, the one that emits `grounding:fa-card-<key>`; a single condition only, MULTI adds nothing) adds that card's key/alias/title terms (`CARD_TOPIC_TERMS`, 32 keys; e.g. jellyfish -> jellyfish sting stung, altitude -> altitude acute mountain sickness hace hape); (b) if t is still empty: the VENOMOUS animal words the detector matched (+ in mode 1 the sting/bite words next to them); COMPOUNDS parts are dropped as in healthTopicTerms; `rs:health-topic-fb:<terms>` is pushed (once) when the set grew.
- Switch `RS_HEALTH_TOPIC_FB`: unset = byte-identical (healthTopicTerms returned as is, no reason code); `1` = spec lists (sting/stung, sickness, mountain, "heart attack", ... included); `2` (**H2b, my addition**) = distinctive terms only (generic words such as sting, sickness, mountain, head, heat dropped).
Deviations from the spec text, all small: (1) the helper lives in its own file (`healthTopicFallback.ts`), not in answer.ts, so the test can import it without loading the engine; `VENOMOUS`, `STUNG_OR_RISK`, `GENERIC_TOPIC`, `COMPOUNDS` are exported from context.ts; (2) the card lookup is `conversationCondition(query, userTurns)` (current turn first, then the user's earlier turns, with the card path's own guards), so a health follow-up with an empty topic also gets its condition; on a single-turn replay it equals `regexCondition(query)`; (3) (b) fires only when a VENOMOUS animal word matched (the raw STUNG_OR_RISK words "bit", "pick", "child" are far too generic: a first version matched "a bit" and "picked" on non-health queries, found by the sibling scan and fixed before any scored run); (4) the test file sits beside the helper (same dir as context.test.ts).

## Safety finding (the reason not to ship this as is)
`replay-sweep-text.ts` (one added field, the final answer text) on vit-hlt-001/002:
- Switch off: both are answered by the reviewed first-aid card (NHS / healthdirect jellyfish card: seawater not fresh water, vinegar in tropical box-jellyfish areas, hot water for pain, call emergency services if ...; altitude card: rest, do not ascend, descend 300-1,000 m, go down at once if confused/unsteady/breathless, ...).
- Switch on (modes 1 and 2): `healthExtractive` finds a source and the card is not used. vit-hlt-001 is answered with a single Wikivoyage excerpt, "If stung by a jellyfish apply vinegar and meat tenderizer. Do not touch irritated skin or wash with fresh water ..." (Gulf Islands National Seashore, a US Gulf Coast page; the Vung Tau chunk with "isopropyl alcohol (not vinegar)" is in the kept list too, as the spec warned, though not the one quoted); vit-hlt-002 is answered with one Wikivoyage sentence about descending at night ("... People with HACE and HAPE ... Help them down!"), without the rest-first rule, the do-not-ascend rule or the red-flag list of the card.
- So FACTPRESENT counts a recovery while the user-visible answer gets worse on both items. The same replacement would hit any card-covered condition whose topic is empty and whose retrieved chunks mention it (the 5 QFORM topic-grown items did not expose it because their kept lists were already non-empty or raw was empty).
- Reader-side warn spec: not exercised here (generation is stubbed and the extractive path returns before the reader); it would apply to a reader-path answer, not to the extractive text above.

## Closest-faithful fix if it is ever revisited (not built, not measured)
Use the fallback topic for the kept list only when no card fires, or keep `grounding:fa-card-<key>` winning over the extractive excerpt when the original topic was empty (card first, sources shown beside it). Without a card, rule (b) alone changed nothing on any dev item. Expected recovery of that variant on dev: 0/25 beyond what the card already gives.
