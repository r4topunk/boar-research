# BONSAI2 — screen of PrismML's ternary Bonsai 2 models (proposed 2026-10-15; for the research session)

Facts (web, 10-15; company-reported unless stated): Bonsai 2 = ternary {−1,0,+1} post-training of Qwen models, Apache 2.0, HF prism-ml/Ternary-Bonsai-2-{2B,4B,9B,27B}-gguf. 27B = Qwen3.8-27B base, PTQ1_0 5.95 GB (1.75 bpw), 14-benchmark avg 84.78 vs FP16 86.32 (98.2 %); knowledge is the weakest category: MMLU-Redux 89.09 vs 91.46, MuSR 70.63 vs 79.63 (card). 9B = Qwen3.5-9B base (+0.46B vision tower), PTQ1_0 2.00 GB / PQ2_0 2.42 GB; no benchmark table on the card ("well over 90 % of FP16 on knowledge/reasoning" in the press release). 4B = 0.95 GB, 2B = 0.43 GB. Runtime: PrismML's llama.cpp fork (branch prism; pre-built binaries incl. Android arm64, macOS arm64, Linux CPU/CUDA); upstream llama.cpp rejects PTQ1_0/PQ2_0 and produces garbage on Q2_0 → llama.rn (the RN app) cannot load them; the native app's JNI llama.cpp could, if built from the fork. Thinking on by default (needs ≥ 8k output tokens; non-thinking: temp 0.7 / top_p 0.8 / top_k 20 / presence 1.5). Phone speed claims: 9B up to 34 tok/s on an iPhone 18 Pro Max (GPU); nothing for Android CPU.

Why it matters: (1) 9B at 2.0 GB vs our 4B Q4_K_M at 2.5 GB: a Qwen3.5-9B that keeps > 90 % of its knowledge would beat Qwen3-4B on the knowledge wall (model size +0.06 measured on 10-06), on the 8 GB tier. (2) 27B dense at 5.95 GB fits the 12 GB tier with room for KV: compare with the deep tier (Qwen3.6-35B-A3B UD-Q2_K_XL 7.9 GB, MoE 3B-active, fast decode) on quality AND decode speed (dense 27B on a phone CPU may be ≈ 1–3 tok/s; the ternary kernels' CPU speed is the unknown). Nothing here ships by 10-20 (engine fork integration); it decides the post-10-20 roadmap and what to tell the app maintainer.
