# boar-visa-en (Wikipedia visa requirements and visa policies)
TL;DR: 387 English Wikipedia articles, "Visa requirements for <nationality> citizens" (203) and "Visa policy of <country>" (184), at the revisions current on 2026-10-01, with every country table flattened to one readable text line per destination; format 2, 11.2 MB; built 2026-10-19; not in any kit; **default-eligible** by the crowding rule (passes by absence); self-test with − without pack +0.007 raw (−0.024…+0.041): the pack text is right but the fast tier rarely retrieves it.

## What it is
- Source: English Wikipedia. Titles = every non-redirect page of the two families in the enwiki-20261001 dump index (651 index titles, 387 articles after dropping redirects). Each article taken at the revision current on 2026-10-01T00:00:00Z and rendered to HTML by the public MediaWiki API (`action=parse&oldid=<rev>`, one request at a time, ≥ 2 s apart). Revision dates: 2026-09 184, 2026-08 92, 2026-07 43, 2026-03…06 63, 2025 5. The dump index carries titles only, so the text came from the API; the snapshot date is kept by pinning the revision.
- Conversion (the pack converter for rendered Wikipedia HTML): headings → section path; paragraphs and lists kept; short-item lists (country lists in policy pages) become one comma-separated line; **each table row becomes one text line**, `<destination>: <status>; Allowed stay: <n days>; Notes: <text>` (rowspans expanded, territory groups as `<country> / <territory>`, the reciprocity tick column dropped). Rows are packed into paragraphs of ≤ 900 chars, each opened by a context line `<article title> (citizens of <country>), <section>:` so every chunk names the nationality and the table. Dropped: maps, images, legends, navigation boxes, footnote markers, reference lists, See also / References / External links. No line starts with `|`, so every chunk is keyword-indexed.
- Example chunk line: `Japan: Visa not required (conditional); Allowed stay: 15 days; Notes: Visa exemption is valid only if registered either through Japanese consulates or through the JAVES online portal.` (Indonesian citizens).
- Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0 (Wikipedia text). Per-row `article_meta.attribution` = `"<title>", Wikipedia contributors, revision <id> of <date> (<url>), CC BY-SA 4.0`; `article_meta.url` = the exact revision URL. Licence file: boar-visa-en.LICENSE.txt (lists all 387 articles with revision URLs). Share-alike: redistributions stay CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Category gaps
- Tables are Wikipedia's crowd-maintained summaries of official rules; values change often (Thailand, Japan, eTA/ETA schemes) and footnoted sources were stripped. Snapshot 2026-10-01: treat as "check before travel".
- Diplomatic/official passport tables and visitor-statistics tables are kept as rows (noise for tourist questions). Policy pages keep passport-type letter codes (e.g. "D O S") without their legend.
- Known conversion defect: in "Visa requirements for Swedish citizens" a malformed row makes later rows (Libya…Marshall Islands) carry a stray "Liberia /" prefix; the destination and values after it are correct.
- No Wikivoyage-style practical advice (border crossings, costs) beyond what the tables' notes hold.
