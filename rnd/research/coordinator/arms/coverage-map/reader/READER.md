## Classes (n 44 unique ids)
- **a_ignored_memory** (12): The fact chunk is kept, usually at pos 2-3 and mostly intact, but the 1.7B anchors on whatever sits at pos 1 and fills the rest from memory. Three sub-patterns. (1) Ordering, in 7 items: pos 1 is a different thread's answer, a film sentence, or an SE question body. The model follows it or cites it, and CT-1 often strips that [n], which releases a warned memory answer (cooking-001, money-018). (2) Numbers and dates, in 3 items (vit-cry-003, lan-rsn-004, lan-exp-004): the figure is in the chunk, but the model writes no number or its own wrong arithmetic. (3) A qualifier is dropped (lan-exp-005), or 'second only to X' is misread (lan-rsn-003). Reconciled ids from the second autopsy pass: vit-hum-004, lan-rsn-001, vit-hlt-003, lan-exp-004 and lan-exp-005 stay a, because the fact is in kept and the answer took pos 1 or memory.
- **f_fact_not_kept (delivery false positive)** (11): The item counts as delivered, but the fact sentence is not in the kept context. This happens in 3 ways. (1) Only the thread's 'Tags:' question chunk is kept and the answer chunks are not; 'delivered' was counted at article level. (2) FACTPRESENT matched on a stray word in an off-topic chunk ('benefit', 'eye', 'algorithmic'). (3) The kit-pack chunk is a stub of 91-216 chars, already that short in raw (vit-cry-002; the second pass said c, but raw was already 216 chars). Head-pass appends from unrelated threads or wiki pages (Sushi history, Cash register, 1998 Russia) add junk. No reader-side switch can fix these: it takes thread-anchored answer appends and a chunk-level delivery metric (retrieval line).
- **f_success_no_harm** (9): The fact is used, and either cited by the model or given its [n] by attributeCitations at 0.75, so no warning. This is the no-harm contrast set. The used-and-cited path works when the fact chunk is present.
- **b_used_uncited_warned** (4): The answer paraphrases or synthesizes kept text, often across 2 chunks (moose plus bison; capital plus euro), but writes no [n]. attributeCitations misses the 0.75 single-source key-term bar, so the compact path releases it with the 'Not found in this phone's offline library. From the model's memory' line. The judge docks the warned version: lan-exp-002 scores 3.5 against ARM-G 4.5 where it cited. cooking-012 also has this b component: it states the 350/400F fact, uncited and warned.
- **e_rubric_mismatch** (4): The fact is present, stated and cited. The judge docks completeness against a 4-point rubric in a 1-2 sentence Succinct answer, or a stale pack figure (Greenland 50k vs 56-57k). This is a ruler or persona issue, not a reader issue.
- **f_se_question_echo** (2): An SE 'Tags:' question-body chunk sits at pos 1, and the model parrots the asker's premise or sentence ('Yes, you can contribute for 2020'; the HIV sentence verbatim). The same thread's answer chunk, which holds the fact, is kept at pos 2 or 5. The engine counts the echo as supported, so the result is an unwarned confident error (CE-unwarned). The same mechanism drives lan-rsn-001 ('more than 6000 km' from a travel-se question) and vit-hlt-003 (a link caption from the question chunk), both filed under a, so 4 items in total and 3 of them unwarned.
- **c_compressed_out** (2): money-016: sentence compression cut an 881-char SE answer chunk to 590 and dropped the '[usage detail removed]K opportunity cost' sentence. money-020: the accepted SE answer (score 12) was in raw but was dropped between raw and kept, by the relative-relevance floor or the topic guard, while generic wiki chunks (Federal Reserve, 1998 Russia) stayed. Only the 236-char question chunk of the thread survived. Secondary cuts appear in cooking-001 (third sentence) and money-018 ('a lease is cheaper only if...').
- **d_se_answer_without_question** (0): None. Every SE answer chunk already carries the thread title as its header ([n] <thread title>). The SE-question-title header fix is therefore not proposed: the opposite problem, the question body acting as a distractor source, is the observed one.
