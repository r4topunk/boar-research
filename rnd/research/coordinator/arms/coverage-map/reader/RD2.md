## Verdict: no
Both bars fail two of the three READS clauses. The CE-unwarned hard gate goes up (18 -> 19 at 0.67 through vit-cry-009; 18 -> 20 at 0.6 through vit-cry-002 and -009: wrong memory answers that share 60-70 % of their key terms with an on-topic EIP chunk lose their warning), and the targets' mean correctness does not move (+0.00 at 0.67 because none of the five targets changed, +0.10 at 0.6). The ΔU clause is met on the changed items (+0.034 at n 3, +0.054 at n 11) but that is the judge crediting the removal of the warning line (usefulness, corr +0.4 at 0.6), the same effect on correct and wrong answers alike, on 3 to 11 items; at 0.67 the CI is over 3 items and means little. Over all 97 the effect is +0.001 / +0.006 and vs the K3 control -0.008 / -0.003 (CI straddles 0). Not READS, not LEVER-CANDIDATE.
## Prediction vs result
Predicted: +0.5 correctness on 2 to 4 of the 5 targets, ΔU +0.02 to +0.04 on the changed set, flips among money-003, vit-cry-001, lan-cmp-004, lan-exp-002. Result: money-003 and lan-exp-002 never flip (support 0.54-0.56 / 0.50 below the bar; the answers are paraphrases of the thread, so lexical support is low), vit-cry-001 and lan-cmp-004 flip only at 0.6 with 0 correctness change (the judge docked the warning, not the content: lan-cmp-004 2.5 -> 2.5, vit-cry-001 2.0 -> 2.0), cooking-012 +0.5. ΔU on changed items was in the predicted range at 0.67 (+0.034), above it at 0.6 (+0.054), but the CE gate was the stated hard risk and it fired.
## What this says
- The warning prefix is not the lever for correctness: where the answer rests on kept text and is right, dropping the warning moves usefulness only; where it is memory, the same rule removes the only honest signal. A lexical support bar cannot separate them (the two wrong EIP answers have the highest support, 0.73 and 0.69; at a bar of 0.75 nothing flips).
- Any further reader-side attempt on cite-or-ground needs a semantic check of the claim (entailment), not term overlap, and the 1.7B is the wrong model to run it; this line stays closed as a post-processing rule.
- Not adopted: a bar tuned on these 11 items (the criterion fixes the rule, one pre-declared raise was allowed and used).
