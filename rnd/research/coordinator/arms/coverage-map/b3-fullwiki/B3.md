## Reads
- Kept facts: shard 00 v0.1 S1 12 -> 9 yet kept 23 -> 23 (+3 / -3: displaced lan-rsn-006, vit-cry-006, vit-cry-011). Across shards S1 drops 1-5 and kept moves -3...+3: coverage without delivery. S3 grows (6 -> 6-12): the fact reaches the candidates and is not kept.
- Crowding: one shard takes 22-37 % of all kept slots (top100k 55 %); every shard but 04/05/10 displaces 1-4 base-kept facts. vit-cry-006 and lan-rsn-006 are each lost in 8 of 15 shards.
- CAVEAT (DEEPWIKI): one shard at a time is not the shipped multi-pack condition. A 15-shard kit stacks 15 competing packs on the same k slots (DEEPWIKI top100k on the deep tier: -0.034); the per-shard kept numbers are therefore optimistic for a full-enwiki kit and the union is an upper bound. The one PASS (shard 13) is the max of 15 noisy draws against a +3 threshold; not a lever without an answer screen, and the criterion sends only PASS builds to Haiku.
- Prediction (<= 2 kept beyond top100k): union has 5 beyond it (cmp-002, cry-001, hum-002, sci-001, money-001), none as a usable single kit.
