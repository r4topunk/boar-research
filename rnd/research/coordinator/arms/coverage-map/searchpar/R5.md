## Engine (patch retrieve-sweep/patches/R5-STICKY.patch, sha 6b172fd2dc95…, tree kiln-local/rs-R5 = rs-H4 + patch; default off)
- Carrier: `ctx.history.rsSticky` = the chunks of the previous turn's `sources` event (what the app already shows; the replay passes the engine's own emitted objects). At most RS_STICKY_MAX = 4. Pack chunks are re-read by id from their pack (original passage, not the compressed copy: new `WikiPack.rsChunkOf`, `packs.rsRehydrate`).
- Placement: after grounding of the base list and before the head pass. They go through compressContext with only the work budget the base list left, then the keep step (onSubject; onHealthTopic for health). So they are pinned-last and cannot displace, reorder or shrink a base chunk (base list is a subset of the arm's list in 100 % of items). `=1` matches against this turn's text; `=2` against previous user question + this turn.
