## Verdict (frozen rule: identity >= 93, facts 42, health 0)
- **IVF32 ADOPT-CANDIDATE** (96/97, 42/42, 0 health; 0.167x kit bytes) = best. **Q4-128 ADOPT-CANDIDATE** (95/97, 42/42, 0; 0.441x).
- Q4-64 NO (92), Q2-128 NO (91), IVF8 NO (90). Facts held 42/42 and health 0/11 in every arm; identity is the only failing gate, as in W78B.
- Reading: the top-50 tail needs about 0.95 recall of the exact set to keep >= 95 % of kept lists; per-row 4-bit codes reach it only with N = 128 (2.6x the pool), 2-bit not at 128; IVF-256 reaches it at nprobe 32 (12.5 % of the lists) because a lead vector's nearest neighbours sit in a few clusters. Travel (Q&A threads, tighter cosines) is the weakest pack under every method.

## Phone reading (not run)
- IVF32 cuts the bytes touched per question, not the resident matrix: this replay still loads the full int8 lead matrix (103.9 MB) and reads only the probed lists. On the phone the win needs lead_vecs stored list-ordered (one contiguous run per list, a pack build step) and read by mmap/offset, so a question pages in about 18 MB instead of 101.75 MB and RSS holds the touched lists; without that the gain is CPU only (stage-1 scan 120 -> 24 ms/q on V8).
- Q4-128 halves both resident RAM (52.99 MB sidecars vs 103.9 MB matrix) and bytes, with a random-row rescore of 128 x 7 rows per question from SQLite (page bound <= 128 x 8 KiB per pack).
- Not measured: IVF32 over the int4 codes (both savings at once), nprobe 16, IVF on the 5 small packs, the 216 QFORM items (W78B part B) under these arms, Hermes timings, PSS. A phone read (9-10 items, POCO X6 Pro) needs the list-ordered pack layout first.
