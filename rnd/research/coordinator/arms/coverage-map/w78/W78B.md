## Verdict (frozen rule): **NO**
Neither N = 32 nor N = 64 passes: part A fails (c) at both and (b) at 32; part B fails (e) and (g) at both and (f) at extra N64. Bytes and scan ms are not the problem (0.126x, stage-1 scan 157 -> 10 ms/q on V8) and the facts that matter hold (42/42; found within one item per form for 3 of 4 arms), but the chunk lists change on 5-16 % of items and the health items move. Cause, post hoc: F1 acts only at cosine >= 0.78, where the true thread is the unique winner and sign-Hamming ranks it in the top 16; denseChunks keeps the top 50 with no floor, a tail ranking where 384 sign bits do not order the articles (top-50 recall 0.30-0.45 at N 32-64; 0.87 at N 512 on MAIN, 0.98 at N 512 on the three small QFORM packs, where N 512 is a third to a half of the rows and part B becomes 216/216 identical, found 69/78 = B, health 0). At N = 32 the pool also shrinks from 50 to 32 articles.
So: **W78 stays an ADOPT-CANDIDATE for the F1 scan only (23.35 -> 2.94 MB/q); the packDense2 stage 1 keeps the int8 matrix.** Whole-kit bytes/q: 125.09 MB now; 104.69 MB with F1 binary only (0.837x); 15.75 MB if both passed (they do not). The dense matrices are 81 % of the bytes (simplewiki alone 80.11 MB/call).
