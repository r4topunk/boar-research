## Method
- The 50 = MAIN 92 keyed minus the 42 chunk-level delivered under SEFIX-124 (sefix/chunklevel-124.json, arm 'sefix F1F2F4'): cm 26 (cooking 12, money 14) + v0.1 24.

Full enwiki is not on disk (B3 deleted the shards), so claims about it are 'not verified'.
- Expected delivered facts = items x 0.54 (PACKPROOF kept rate) x 0.65 (read right when kept).

## Caveats
- Probe regexes are hand-written per item; a hit means the fact string sits in one chunk of that pack, not that retrieval can find it. Snippets were read by hand; vital5's vit-sci-002 hit was spurious (Aomori) and is not counted.
- (c) for cm items is by construction (self-tests written from those packs). For v0.1 several (c) facts are split across two articles (lan-rsn-002, lan-cmp-001) or need synthesis/arithmetic (lan-cmp-003, vit-sci-002, vit-cry-009); vit-cry-005 and vit-cry-010 are partial in boar-crypto.
- The 0.54 x 0.65 multipliers come from pack-matched self-tests (PACKPROOF, 1.7B Succinct, Haiku labels); general questions may deliver less (B3/top100k).
- Philosophy-pack size and the title-list path are UNKNOWN (no category dump on disk); effort is an estimate from B3's per-shard cost (~205 s/shard).
