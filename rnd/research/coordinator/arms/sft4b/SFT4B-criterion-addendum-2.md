## Data v3 (replaces v1/v2 as the training set; same disjoint article pool, same 84-article hold-out)
- **Context:** the engine's real rendering (stage-0 render_prompt.py) of **3–6 passages**: the gold chunk(s) of the article plus **2–5 distractor passages** obtained from the engine's own retrieval for the written question (the pack installed with the kit, raw top-6; distractors = the non-gold hits; if retrieval yields < 2 distractors, add BM25 neighbours from the same pack). Gold position random. **Gold present in ≈ 80 % of examples; ≈ 20 % gold-absent** (distractors only).
- **Targets (40–60 words):** gold-present → the fact plus one supporting detail, citing every passage actually used in the engine's citation style; **≥ 30 % of gold-present examples use two gold chunks of the same article (cite two sources)**. Gold-absent → the answer the claim build gives when no passage covers the question: an honest "the sources shown do not cover this" sentence in the engine's own phrasing (copy it from real cut-7 traces) followed by a brief general answer, with no citation of a distractor.
- **0-call gates before training:** ≥ 1,000 kept; median target ≥ 35 words; ≥ 30 % of gold-present targets cite > 1 source; jev round-1 pass ≥ 50 % of checked answers (the stage-0 basis); gold-absent share 15–25 %; a strict 20-example hold-out review by the coordinator's reviewer agent ≤ 6/20 unsupported (the CLOSED R12 human-baseline bar).
- Hold-out v3 = the same 84 articles rendered the same way (gold-present/absent mix as train).
