## C. Methodology
**C1. Multiplicity.** CLOSED.md has 97 rows, the ledger audit counted 154 arm records by 10-13, and there are 209 entries under arms/. Of the judged comparisons (≈ 60), about 5 are LEVER-class: X1/X2b, LITE5, DEEPTHINK, QUANTCEIL-type cuts, VENUEGPS (plumbing). If all ~60 had no effect, a one-sided α 0.025 rule would yield ≈ 1.5 false "CI low > 0".
- The large levers survive even Bonferroni over 100: X2b z ≈ 5.5, LITE5-180 z ≈ 6, DEEPTHINK z ≈ 4.2.
- Multiplicity is **not** the main problem; selection on the same dev sets is.
  - The deep config was picked on V-para.
  - X2b was designed on the 180.
  - The SMALLROUTE and DEEPROUTE thresholds were swept on the evaluation items.
- BACKLOG §0's own pipeline metric reads "0 V-para floor-adj points adopted per 1,180 judge calls".

**C2. Dev-set reuse.**
- v0.2: burnt (git d8154b6) and spent 12+ full runs.
- V-para: 19 full runs + 12 slices + 4 anchors + 11 venue passes (EXPOSURES status table).
- 180 self-tests: written by Claude from pack text, then used to design and to judge X1/X2/X2b/LITE*.
- No unexposed set exists (es-fresh2 is clean but in Spanish and parked).
- Every "PASS/LEVER" since 10-06 is dev data. The claim draft says this; the summaries usually do not.

**C3. In-sample oracles presented as ceilings.**
- SMALLROUTE ceilings are max(two noisy draws), so they are inflated by judge noise.
- LITE8 / KIT-X2b / DETFP / HEALTHDROP close on "ceiling < MDE". Those ceilings come from jev- or agent-labelled loss buckets (LITE6, LOSSMAP), which are unvalidated label models.
- ORACLE-ST +0.095 ("RETRIEVAL-HEADROOM") is an oracle with the gold chunk appended. It is a real upper bound, but only for questions written from a chunk.

**C4. MDE vs CI widths.**
- CLOSED.md lists 21 closures on n ≈ 20 slices (MDE ≈ 0.08 by its own TL;DR).
- At least 6 of these have a CI upper end ≥ +0.03, so they cannot exclude a lever: R10 +0.068, R25 +0.048, R31 +0.048, R83 +0.038, R84 +0.063, R85 +0.066, R97 +0.038. They should read "not detected", not "closed".
- On the 180, the CI half-width is ≈ 0.023 → MDE ≈ 0.033, as stated.

**C5. Seed noise.**
- SEEDVAR: the same config (H) on two seeds gives paired Δ **−0.039 (−0.072…−0.011)** on V-para (seedvar-H-3seed.opus.json).
- So the standing rule "a one-seed deep Δ on V-para < 0.03 is seed noise" is too lax; seed noise reached 0.039 with a CI that excludes 0. The rule should be ≥ 0.05 or two seeds.
- The lite and 180 arms are all one seed (42).
- LITE5-180's same-host anchor re-sampled 2/20 answers with identical inputs, so llama-server is not deterministic either.

## E. What the program is probably right about
- **The bar is not met by anything shippable, and the claim text says so.** Win 0/51 on V-para for every config and seed is robust to every critique above; even a +0.1 judge leniency would not produce wins.
- **Reach dominates the 4B's loss on pack questions, and X1/X2b is a real mechanism.** The split design is good: +0.177 where the fact newly arrives, ≈ 0 for non-fact appends, no harm on v0.1, kit byte-identical. Effects of +0.066 with z ≈ 5.5 do not come from multiplicity.
- **Deep + thinking is the largest measured lever** (+0.17 floor-adj over the 4B on V-para, two seeds, Opus). The budget-curve knee at 512 is well supported (1024 parity, 256 loses, 3072 no gain).
- **The discipline is unusual and mostly real:** frozen hashes (18/19 match), 0-call gates before judge spend, an exposure ledger, self-corrections logged (29 CORRECTION rows), and negative results recorded (97 CLOSED rows). WARN-OOS/WARN-DET were run precisely to catch in-sample over-fit, and they did.
- **The floor-adjusted headline is the conservative choice**, and the judge is test-retest stable for Opus (JUDGEVAR σ ≈ 0.003).
