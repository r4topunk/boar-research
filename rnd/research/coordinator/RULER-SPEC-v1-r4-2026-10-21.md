## 2. Units, weights and sets
### 2.1 Units
- **Unit** = one item. A single-turn item is one answer. A multi-turn item (8) is one conversation, run turn by turn. The judge sees the whole conversation and scores the item once (§6.3). A refused turn stays in the transcript as the refusal text. The item is zeroed as a whole only if every turn refused [F11].

### 2.2 Sets and weights
- **Headline set H** = the 71 items with `tags.anchor_vexact=false`. The 4 V-exact anchors are scored by the same pipeline and printed in their own row. The co-row **H ∪ anchors** (75) is printed beside the headline, which may not appear without it [F15].
- **Headline weight** w_i = 0.5 if `tags.health_safety=true` (11 items), else 1 [r4to-4]. **Weight-1 variant** w1_i = 1. Both are computed for every weighted metric. Σ_H w = 65.5.
- No stratum reweighting [R-D4]; the effective weight share of each `type` and `family` is printed.

### 2.3 Breakdown rows (each with its own CI, never replacing the headline)
By `type` [B31:27]; non-lookup types pooled (flagged if its CI low ≤ 0.50 while the headline passes [F22]); by `family`; by `writer`; judge family × writer family [F5]; by `situation`; time_sensitive excluded vs included [R-D4]; multi-turn alone; over_refusal items (refusal rate); plus two rows new in r3:
- **Current-city stratum (n 7)** [RV4 §3]: the 7 items with a device location (all travel, weight 1, 7/65.5 = 10.7 % of headline weight). Printed: U-total **with** the 7 (= the headline, scored as given) and **without** them. The verdict uses "with". The headline row states plainly if the RC has no location or places path ("cannot answer current-city questions: 7 of 71 items, 10.7 % of headline weight"). If the two U-totals fall on different sides of 0.50, the verdict sentence names both (with the 7 at zero this happens iff U-without is in 0.50–0.56; with non-zero scores it can also happen from below, RV4: 0.44–0.56) [G8]. One labelled sensitivity row, "with device location + area tile (desktop runner, not the submitted APK)", may be printed. It is never the headline.
- **In-pack stratum** [INPACK rule INPACK-R1 (renamed from "R1" to avoid a clash with RULING R1) [G15], frozen with its JSON and script shas in §10.3]: U on the INPACK-R1 in-pack ids and on the off-pack ids (in H: 55 in-pack, 16 off-pack; 57 / 18 over H ∪ anchors) [G8], each against its own reference denominator, split per family (off-pack is almost all humanities, so a pooled in-vs-off gap is a family gap). Every in-pack number carries the caveat "domain match, not fact presence", and is never shown without the off-pack and all-item U. Also printed: A∪ − B on in-pack ids, and A∪ − B on off-pack ids (expected ≈ 0; if not, crowding or a prompt effect, not a pack effect), both with the common denominator of §3.4.

### 2.4 Information-available split (FACT-AVAIL) [r4]
- **Why**: an offline app can only answer from what is installed. U-total mixes two different failures: the packs do not contain the answer (a content gap, known in advance), and the packs contain it but the app does not deliver it (an app failure). The split reports them apart [r4to-C1].
- **Label** (ruler/factavail/FACT-AVAIL-criterion.md, sha b9527555…, frozen before any call and before any app answer): for each checklist point, candidate passages from the six A∪ text packs (lexical FTS5 search, ≤ 24 passages) are read by a labeller (Haiku 5.5-low, validated against Opus on 40 points; Opus labels all points if agreement < 34/40) that answers whether a passage states the point. share_i = points stated / K_i. **AVAILABLE** share ≥ 0.5; **PARTIAL** 0 < share < 0.5; **NONE** share = 0. The supporting passages (pack, chunk id, title) are stored per stated point.
- Properties, stated with every number: (a) the label is a **lower bound** (a fact in a passage the lexical search missed counts as absent); (b) it concerns the **A∪ packs**, so for arm B (no topical packs) it describes what A∪ would add, not what B has; the RC's built-in knowledge base is not labelled (its text is not on disk; UNKNOWN); (c) it is fixed before any app answer and never revised after; (d) no app output, trace or score enters it.
- **Label per installed set** [H1]: the stored supporting passages carry their pack id, so the label for any installed subset S is recomputed mechanically (no new calls): share_i(S) = points with a supporting passage from a pack in S / K_i. The verdict line uses S = the packs actually installed on the verdict arm (§4.2). For arm B (no A∪ pack) the line reads: "No A∪ pack is installed; the built-in knowledge base is not labelled; on the N_avail(A∪) items that A∪ would cover, B scores U = x."
- **Quarantine** [H2]: the labels file, its NONE/PARTIAL lists and the supporting-passage lists are coordinator-only, handled like blind/ material. No pack-building, retrieval or engine agent and no maintainer note reads them, and they are not published before the result card. The A∪ pack shas are frozen in persona-map-v1 (§10.3) for v0.4. Any pack built or changed after the labels exist makes a new map and a new claim labelled "developed after exposure"; its U-avail is printed beside the original, never instead of it. One EXPOSURES.md row is written when the labels file is created.
- **Not a with/without contrast** [H3]: U-avail vs U-none compares different items (NONE is dominated by families the packs do not cover) and is not an estimate of the information effect. The same-item contrast is ORACLE − ORACLE-0 (§4.1).
- Item-level label agreement (Haiku vs Opus) on the items of the 40 validated points is printed beside the strata [H7].
- Reported for every phone arm, each against its own reference mass on the same ids (§3.4), per judge family: **U-avail** = U_g(X, default, AVAILABLE), **U-partial**, **U-none** = U_g(X, default, NONE), counts n per stratum, and the same three for the reference absolute means. U-total remains the headline and verdict metric; the split is never printed without it.
- **Delivery decomposition on AVAILABLE items** (per arm, unweighted counts, per judge family) [H5]: (1) **title not retrieved**: no supporting passage's title is among the RC's retrievedTitles for the item; (2) **title retrieved, not correct** (k = 0 or refused); (3) **title retrieved and correct** (k = 1); (4) **ambiguous**: a supporting title also present in the built-in KB, excluded from (1)–(3). "Title retrieved" does not mean the supporting chunk was kept; bucket (2) is not attributed to reading. Plus refusals on AVAILABLE items ("refused although the information was installed") and confident errors on NONE items ("answered without the information and wrong").
- Prior to keep in view (dev, FACTPRESENT-2026-10-21): on real v0.1 items, when a pack put the key fact into the kept context, the 1.7B's U did not measurably rise (−0.010, CI −0.077…+0.050, n 10) (post hoc, Haiku-low judgments; on the topical self-tests the same cell gave +0.139 [+0.075, +0.201], n 27) [H8]. The split may therefore show a low U-avail; that is a reading result, and the ORACLE row (§4.1) tells reading from retrieval.

## 3. Metrics (exact formulas)

### 3.1 Per-unit judge quantities (judge.v1 + DECLINE2STAGE) [R1, D2S]
For item i, answer a, judge family g in {G1 = Claude (Opus), G2 = Codex}, reference family f in {F1 = Claude, F2 = Codex}:
- **Stage 2, judge.v1** (bytes of boar-eval scorers/vendor/eval-lib/judge-core.mjs unchanged): a pairwise call scores answer a and reference ref_f on correctness, completeness and usefulness, 1–5 each, in both A/B orders. The first order per item is seeded (`firstOrders(ids, 7)`). Per order, m(·) = the mean of the three rubric scores. Per pairing, the scores are averaged over the two orders: m_g(a | ref_f, i) and m_g(ref_f | a, i).
- **Stage 1, DECLINE2STAGE**: one call per answer text (question and answer only) asks the frozen question "Does the answer attempt to answer the question (give any substantive claim about it)? yes/no". If the label is "no", the answer is a **non-answer** (n(a,i) = 1). Prompt, model and settings as in D2S (verbatim in §6.2).
- **Usefulness mass** of an answer: u_g(a,i) = 0 if n(a,i) = 1 or z(a,i) = 1 (below), else the mean over f of [m_g(a | ref_f, i) − 1], in [0, 4].
- **Reference mass** [G2]: for arm X, r_g(X,i) = the mean, over reference families f and over the generations of X that were scored, of [m_g(ref_f | X_gen, i) − 1], taken from X's own pairings (as judge-core `summarize` does). **Stage 2 always runs, including for answers with z = 1 or n = 1**, so that r is defined. u and r of the same generation are resampled together in the bootstrap. References are never stage-1 zeroed; a reference that stage 1 calls a non-answer is listed and counted, with no other action. Where this spec writes r_g(i) without an arm, it means r_g(X,i) of the arm in question.
- **Refusal** z(a,i) = 1 iff the RC's trace marks the answer declined OR the text equals the RC's fixed refusal string (exact bytes in §10.6). Then u := 0 for **app arms** regardless of both stages (true zero) [r4to-1]. The z override does not apply to the decline arm, which is always judged (§4) [F2]. Stage 1 and z are logged separately, so a refusal that z misses but stage 1 catches is visible.
- **Generations**: for an arm with two generations, u_g(X,i) is the mean of the two generations' u; m and the indicators below are computed per generation and averaged.
- **Correct** k_g(a,i) = 1 iff the mean correctness over the two orders and two pairings is ≥ 4 and n = z = 0. **Confident error** x_g(a,i) = 1 iff n = z = 0 and the mean correctness ≤ 1.5 (the CE label of D2S).
- **Dangerous** d_g(a,i): a separate harm call (§7.1 safety gate, protocol in §6.5).
- The warning line of a "show anyway" answer (exact bytes pre-registered from the RC) is stripped before both stages and its presence is logged [ours].

### 3.2 Reference denominator [R-D2, REFVAR]
- Two references per item, one per family (F1 Claude Opus + web, F2 Codex + web), one draw each, uncapped, sources kept, generated and archived **before any app arm generates any v0.4 answer** [F16]. REFVAR measured two independent Opus draws moving U by 0.015 (1.7B) and 0.008 (4B), within judge test-retest noise, and found the uncapped reference does not lower U (+0.013 / +0.001 vs the 250-word v1) (Haiku-low judge, 20 v0.1 dev items, both draws Opus; the variance between reference families is unmeasured) [G8]. So one draw per family is used, and the two-family mean is the denominator.
- The reference mass is the mean over the two families, never the per-item max or min [R-D2; F13]. Each family alone is a sensitivity row. REFVAR measured within-family draw noise only (≤ 0.015); **a difference between the two one-family rows is reported as a family difference, not as noise** [G8].
- Protocol: ruler/refs-v2/REFS-V2-criterion.md (sha 65e84704…), frozen 2026-10-21 20:36 and used from 20:37, before this spec's final sha; §10's order is stated as it happened [G12]. Message frame (verbatim): "Current date: YYYY-MM-DD" (the call's date, GMT-3); for location items "The user is currently in <city> (coordinates <lat>, <lon>)."; for turn k > 1 "Conversation so far:" (user turns + that family's own earlier answers) then "User's latest message:". F1 = claude-opus-5-5 via `claude -p` + WebSearch/WebFetch; F2 = gpt-5.6-sol via `codex exec`, reasoning high, web_search live.
- **Contrast check** (context, 0 calls, before freeze) [G2]: the reference-side mean on existing dev judgments when paired with FLOOR decline texts vs with MAIN control answers on the same ids is printed here when computed; in v0.4 tables each arm's reference mass on refused vs answered items is printed.
- Printed beside every ratio: the count of items with r_g(i) = 0 and the share of numerator mass from those items [F9].
- **Contested points** [REFVAR §4.4]: after both references exist and before any app answer, a coordinator agent reads, for each item, the two references against the item's checklist (0 app text exists at that time). A point is **contested** only if two independent agents (one Claude, one Codex), each reading the two references and the checklist, both mark it incompatible [G7]. Contested points are removed from the author notes, and the notes get one fixed line per contested point: "Sources disagree on <point>: '<value F1>' vs '<value F2>'. Do not penalise either value." The list is the **errata file** (§10.2), with both references' quoted values, hashed before any judgment. Its size is published. No point's wording is changed. Sensitivity row: U-total with the original notes (no errata), re-judged on the affected items only.
- **As-of date**: the reference message carries "Current date: YYYY-MM-DD" and the archive stores the generation timestamp. Time-sensitive facts that changed after the pack snapshots are a known handicap of an offline app; it is reported (time-sensitive row), not corrected.

### 3.3 Coverage [r4to-1]
For arm X and item i, from the RC's own run-time trace:
- kept_X(i, turn) = 1 iff the answer step **received** at least one kept source record (pack chunk, knowledge-base passage, places record or deterministic fact-table row), whether or not the output cites it. The trace field is pinned in §10.6. On the native hook today this is `retrievalUsed` / non-empty `retrievedTitles` (GAP row 6: passages with similarity ≥ 0.7 only); if the RC's field means something else, the card says so.
- Single-turn: cov_X(i) = kept_X(i). Multi-turn: 1 if every turn kept, 0 if none kept, else **mixed**. If a UI-driven turn has no trace, that item's coverage is "unknown"; it is counted in M_X and listed [G10].
- C_X (covered), N_X (uncovered), M_X (mixed); **coverage** c_X = |C_X| / |H| (also weighted c^w_X); |N_X| and |M_X| printed beside it [F11]. No item label, tag or judge verdict enters coverage.
- **Support coverage** (reported, never used to route, select, drop or weight) [coordinator input 2026-10-20]: needs the kept chunks' **text** in the trace. Per judge family, a support call reads the kept text of item i (turns pooled) and the item's checklist (minus contested points) and labels each point stated / not stated; σ_g(X,i) = stated / K_i; S_X,g = {i in C_X : σ ≥ 0.5}. Published: |S|/|H|, mean σ over C_X, share of C_X with σ = 0. **If the RC trace has no kept text (true of the native hook today, §4.3), support coverage is "not measurable on this RC" and is not replaced by a bench-app replay in the headline table** (a labelled replay row may be printed). Support prompt frozen in §10.4.

### 3.4 Usefulness ratios (ratio of sums; one per judge family g)
For subset S and answers of arm X in mode m (default or show-anyway):
U_g(X,m,S) = Σ_{i in S} w_i · u_g(X_m, i) / Σ_{i in S} w_i · r_g(X,i); n/a if the denominator is 0 [F9]. U uses the floor-adjusted scale (m − 1); the raw `qualityRatio` that judge.mjs prints is never quoted as U [G15].
- **Cross-arm comparisons** [G2] (A∪ − B, ORACLE − ORACLE-0, ORACLE − A∪ [r4], pack lift, Δ_D, in-pack and off-pack Δ, separability) use a common per-item denominator r̄_g(X,Y,i) = ½[r_g(X,i) + r_g(Y,i)] for both arms of the pair: paired Δ = Σ w (u_X − u_Y) / Σ w r̄. Each arm's own U (and the verdict) keeps its own r. Every table prints each arm's mean reference mass Σ w r / Σ w, and its mean reference mass on refused vs answered items (the contrast check).
- **U-ref** = U_g(X, default, C_X). **U-noref, default** = U_g(X, default, N_X). **U-noref, show anyway** = U_g(X, show, N_X), where X_show is the separately logged answer after the "show anyway" control (§4.3 says how it is produced on the RC).
- **U-total** = U_g(X, default, H). Exact decomposition, printed in numerator form [F9]: U-total = Σ_{S in {C,N,M}} [Σ_S w_i u_g(X,i)] / Σ_H w_i r_g(i). The item-count form c·U-ref + (1−c)·U-noref is not the total and is never printed as one.
- **U-total, show anyway** (sensitivity) = U_g over H using the show-anyway answer on every item the RC refused and the default answer elsewhere.
- **Correct-rate ratio** CR_g(X) = Σ_H w_i k_g(X,i) / Σ_H w_i k_g(ref,X,i), where k_g(ref,X,i) = the mean over f and over X's scored generations of the reference's correct indicator in X's pairings; printed per arm with that arm's reference term [G2]. It replaces r2's task-success ratio, which needed a judge field judge.v1 does not have [R1].
- Health variants: every weighted ratio with w and with w1, side by side [r4to-4].
- Absolute means (Σ_H w (m−1) / Σ_H w, on the 0–4 scale) for the app and the references are printed beside each ratio [G15].
- **Judge-offset sensitivity rows** [R1, G3]: (a) judge.v1 alone, without the stage-1 zero; (b) U_net,g = (U_v1,g − D_g) / (1 − D_g), where D_g is the judge.v1-alone U of decline variant (a) under family g on the v0.4 decline arm (§4.1). Haiku's 0.058 is quoted only for the Haiku diagnostic scale. The verdict sentence names the U_net band when it differs from the headline band (§8). If the RC1 stage-1 audit (§6.2) fails, the roles swap: the headline is judge.v1 + U_net,g and stage 1 becomes sensitivity row (a').

### 3.5 Pack lift and interference [r4to-3]
- Arm C answer: C_ans(i) = C's show-anyway answer if C refused item i, else C's default answer (c_C reported) [F14].
- **Scale** [G9]: pack lift, interference and displacement are computed on the Haiku-L1 diagnostic scale only (A, C, D are judged by Haiku-low only) and labelled so.
- **Pack lift** on items A∪ covers: L = Σ_{C_A∪} w_i [u(A∪,i) − u(C_ans,i)] / Σ_{C_A∪} w_i r̄(A∪,C,i) (common denominator, §3.4). Also published: U_g(C_ans, H) (the model alone) and U_g(C_ans, C_A∪). The same lift for A and B.
- **Interference**: paired Δ_D = Σ_H w (u(D,i) − u(A,i)) / Σ_H w r̄(D,A,i) (common denominator, §3.4), the same Δ on C_A, and c_D − c_A.
- **Displacement case** (from traces): i in C_A, a source kept in A absent from D's kept set, and at least one D-kept source from a pack in D \ A. Marked **harm (diagnostic)** only if u(D,i) < u(A,i) under Haiku-low [G9]. Needs per-source pack identity in the trace; if the RC trace has titles only, the list is built from titles and labelled.

### 3.6 Error and comparison rates
Unweighted on purpose (counts a user sees), each also in the weighted form [F13].
- **Confident-error rate** CE_g(X,m) = |{i in H : n = z = 0, x_g = 1}| / |{i in H : n = z = 0}|, with the answered count beside it, and **CE over H** = |{i in H : x_g = 1}| / |H|, so refusing more cannot hide in CE [F9, F23]. For default mode, show-anyway answers alone (warned), and each reference.
- **Win/tie/loss** per judge family against each reference family separately: the combined winner of the two orders of that pairing (agreement keeps the verdict, disagreement = tie; judge-core `combineOrders`); an answer with z = 1 or n = 1 is a loss, unless the reference is also a stage-1 non-answer (then a tie) [G15]. Win-rate = W/|H| and (W + T/2)/|H|. Reported, never the threshold [R-D1; F13].
- **Refusal rate**, **over-refusal rate** (refusals on `over_refusal=true` items), **stage-1 non-answer rate** (n = 1 among z = 0 answers).
- Faithful-refusal and answered-without-support rates (r2 §3.6) are reported only where support coverage is measurable (§3.3).

### 3.7 Uncertainty and noise [r4to-5]
- CI for every ratio and Δ: paired item bootstrap (numerator and denominator resampled together; all arms and both judge families share the same item resample per draw), B = 2000, seed 11, percentiles 2.5/97.5. Each draw also resamples, per item, the two A/B orders (the judge's own repeat) and the generation seeds where two exist (§3.7 next bullet), so judge order noise and generation noise are inside the CI [F12]. Zero-denominator draws are redrawn and counted.
- **Generation noise**: A∪ and B run **two generations per item** (seeds 42 and 43 if the hook takes a seed; else two independent runs, labelled "seed not controllable"); they are the first two complete runs of each arm [G5]; the verdict uses the per-item mean of u (and of r, §3.1) over the two. Other arms run one.
- **Completion** [G11]: both judge families must complete the verdict arm (A∪, or the installable arm of §4.2) and B on both generations; otherwise the card reads **"judge incomplete"** and no band is printed. A single family is never substituted.
- **Test-retest noise figure** per judge family, printed next to every ratio: a second full judge pass (new process, same inputs, order seed 8) on a pre-registered **15-item retest subset** (ids drawn by sha-ordering from H, stratified by family, listed in §10.4) for A∪ seed 42. Printed: (a) per-item score flip rate (share of items whose u moves by ≥ 0.5 between passes), (b) |ΔU| between passes on the subset, (c) the judge-only CI half-width (bootstrap over orders with items fixed).
- **Verdict statistic** = min(U_G1, U_G2), computed inside each bootstrap draw; the verdict uses its point estimate and its 2.5 percentile [R-D1, F20]. Every ratio is printed with its judge family name. The Claude judge is known to be stricter than the OpenAI one by 0.10–0.14 (floor-adjusted) on dev sets (XFAM, XFAM-V02) [G8]; the min rule is therefore expected to be the Claude number, which is why it is the rule.

## 6. Judge protocol [R1, r4to-5, r4to-B2]
### 6.1 Stage 2: judge.v1
- Code: boar-eval harness/judge.mjs (Claude family, Opus, judgeVersion judge.v1) and harness/judge-codex.mjs (Codex family, judgeVersion judge.v1-codex, reasoning effort high), both importing judge-core.mjs unchanged. Diagnostic: judge.mjs `--effort low --judge-model haiku` (judge.v1-low).

### 6.2 Stage 1: DECLINE2STAGE (verbatim, frozen by D2S)

- Evidence (dev sets, D2S): 90/90 frozen decline texts flagged (30/30 on the fluent no-content variant); 0 of 47 MAIN non-refusal answers with Opus judge.v1 correctness ≥ 3 zeroed (and 0 of 27 WARN); 20/20 retest identical; on the v0.1 ladder pairs move by at most 0.004 (135 of the ladder texts used a 0-call stand-in labeller, not stage 1) [G8]. Bar (b) FAILED by its frozen count (2 > 1). Both hits are the engine's fixed refusal string, which stage 1 labels correctly. RULING R1 (coordinator, after the result) overrode the frozen kill clause and adopted the two-stage judge. The independent reviewer accepted the override with conditions (RULER-SPEC-v1-r3-REVIEW G3). The first fallback (JUDGE-V2.1-NI) was not run [G3].
- **RC1 stage-1 audit** (condition of G3; criterion frozen before the calls; §10.4): stage 1 on the 51 RC1 rc-cv5-51-15b answers (dev, 51 Haiku calls). Bar: ≤ 1 "not attempted" among answers that are not the RC's refusal text and have Opus judge.v1 correctness ≥ 3. If it fails, the headline is judge.v1 + U_net,g and stage 1 becomes a sensitivity row (§3.4). **Result 2026-10-21: PASS** (0 of 51 marked not-attempted; 0 of the 2 with correctness ≥ 3; low power, see ruler/rc1-stage1/RC1-STAGE1-2026-10-21.md).
- **One stage 1 for both judge families.** Stage 1 is a non-answer gate, not a quality score. It is the validated Haiku classifier for both families, stated as a shared Claude-family component. A Codex stage 1 is not used, because it has no validation run. Sensitivity row (a) of §3.4 shows each family's U without stage 1.
- For multi-turn items stage 1 runs per turn, and for turn k it receives as the question the numbered user turns 1..k (an unvalidated variant of the frozen prompt, labelled; its non-answer count on multi-turn items is printed apart) [G10]. The item's u is zeroed only if every turn is a non-answer or refusal (§2.1); otherwise refused/non-answer turns stay in the transcript, marked.

## 11. How a reader could call this cherry-picking, and why it is not
| Charge | Answer |
|---|---|
| "You chose packs per question." | The verdict arm installs every persona pack once for every item. The map comes from pack cards and five family tags, written without item text, approved independently, hashed before any answer. B is always printed beside it. |
| "You score the app with packs it does not ship." | If the RC cannot install A∪ through its own user flows, the verdict is written on what a user can install (A∪-partial or B), and A∪ is marked "not scored" (§4.2). Packs are never side-loaded by a path a user does not have, and a pack counts as installed only if retrieval uses it. |
| "Your judge gives credit to refusals." | Non-answers get a true zero from a separate classifier (90/90 decline texts flagged; 0 non-refusal answers zeroed among 74 dev answers that Opus scored correctness ≥ 3), adopted by a coordinator override of a frozen FAIL that the independent reviewer accepted with conditions, including an audit on the native app's own answers (PASS, 0 of 51 zeroed, but low power: only 2 of those answers were scored correct enough to test the bar). The decline arm must score ≤ 0.05 under both families on every variant, or no band is printed. The no-stage-1 number and the U_net band are printed. |
| "The coverage split hides the bad half." | U-total over all items is the verdict metric and never appears without its parts. Coverage is read from the RC's own trace. |
| "Health is down-weighted." | Decided before any answer; weight-1 U always printed; a band change between weightings is named; one dangerous answer fails the build. |
| "You only report the questions your packs cover." [r4] | The split is labelled before any answer, from pack text alone, never revised; U-total over all items stays the verdict and is printed first; U-none and the share of items with no installed answer are always printed beside U-avail. |
| "You dropped the city questions the app cannot answer." | They stay in the headline, scored as given; a with/without row shows their effect (about 0.1 U: 10.7 % of headline weight; dev proxy 9.8 %). |
| "The judge is your own family, and it wrote the items." | Two families (Claude, Codex); the verdict takes the lower inside each draw; the writer × judge table shows self-preference. |
| "The reference is weak or shaped." | Uncapped, live web, sources kept, two families, mean per item, each family alone shown; archived before any app answer; contested points dropped by a rule, not by reading app answers. |
| "You re-ran until it looked good." | Map and errata are frozen once for v0.4; each RC's state is registered before its first run; aborted runs are kept and logged; later RCs print every earlier result with the attempt count. |
| "You measured a desktop." | The verdict arm runs on the RC on the phone; anything else is labelled. |
