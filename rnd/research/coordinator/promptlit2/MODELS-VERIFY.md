# MODELS-VERIFY: primary-source check of the four model sweeps + one comparison table

TL;DR: The sweeps' vendor numbers mostly hold up. Three of their "conflicts" are really different benchmark versions or modes. **Qwen3-1.7B BFCL:** 56.6 is v3 thinking and 52.2 is v3 non-thinking (both from the tech report), 54.70 is the official v3-era leaderboard snapshot, and 28.41 is the current BFCL **v4** leaderboard. **LFM2.5 BFCL:** the card's two tables disagree with each other (v3 64.36 vs 64.79, v4 48.50 vs 49.73), and neither gives a harness or mode. **Qwen3.6 BFCL-V4 57.83:** this is Red Hat's own FC run with thinking on, not a Qwen or leaderboard figure. On the one comparable tool benchmark (official BFCL v4 leaderboard, FC mode), Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 scores 35.68 and Qwen3-1.7B 28.41. Both score ≈ 3 % on the web-search (multi-hop) category. Our own measurements still rank Qwen3-4B first among the phone-class models.

Date 2026-10-19. Verifier: Opus 5.5. Method: I downloaded raw HF READMEs, chat templates and tokenizer configs; the arXiv HTML (2505.09388, 2511.22138); the BFCL leaderboard's own CSV (`gorilla.cs.berkeley.edu/data_overall.csv`, page "Last Updated: 2026-04-12", bfcl-eval 2025.12.17) and its historical snapshots (github HuanzhiMao/BFCL-Result); the Liquid blog HTML; the Red Hat and Qualcomm HF cards; and llama.cpp source at tag **b11010** (`common/chat.cpp`, `common/arg.cpp`) via `gh api`. I read the Artificial Analysis pages with WebFetch. Inputs: model-qwen3-4b-2507.md, model-qwen3-1.7b.md, model-lfm25-8b-a1b.md, model-qwen36-35b-a3b.md. I cite VERIFY.md and MINICPM-VERIFY.md without redoing them.

Verdicts: CONFIRMED / CORRECTED / OVERSTATED / NOT FOUND. "Vendor" means self-reported.

## 6. Bottom line for an agentic offline research assistant over knowledge packs

- **Try Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 first for tool calling over packs.** It is the only phone-class model with an official BFCL v4 FC score that beats the 1.7B (35.68 vs 28.41; irrelevance 84.9 vs 76.5). It has no hidden reasoning tokens. On our record it has the best measured quality and the lowest wrong-claim rate (3/73 and 1/66 vs LFM 7/58), and it already runs on our phone at p50 31 s.
- **Use a short, engine-driven loop, not open multi-hop search.** On BFCL v4 the 4B and 1.7B score 3.0 and 2.5 on web search and 22.1 and 11.0 on multi-turn, against 87.9 and 82.9 on single-call AST. The engine should own the loop and the model should emit one or two well-formed calls per turn: query in, pack hits back, then answer.
- **First check is parsing, not quality.** At b11010 the Qwen3 Hermes `<tool_call>` JSON has no dedicated parser and falls back to the generic autoparser. LFM2.5, Qwen3.6 (Qwen3-Coder XML) and MiniCPM5 each have a dedicated branch. A 0-judge smoke test should log the parse rate on our own tool schema before any graded run.
- **Gate distractor tools.** The 2507 Instruct misuses an offered but irrelevant tool 24.0 % of the time, against 3.4 % for invented tools (VERIFY). Offer only the pack-search tools relevant to the turn, and include no-call and distractor items in the test set.
- **LFM2.5-8B-A1B: do not switch on vendor numbers.** Its BFCL figures are vendor-only, disagree with each other and use an unstated harness. OpenBMB's reproduction (v4 49.2) is the only cross-check. Its high Omniscience score comes from abstaining, at 8.67 % accuracy. On our phone turns it made more wrong claims, refused more and ran slower (40 s vs 27 s), and its reasoning cannot be switched off. Keep it as a challenger only through PACKREAD/PROMPT3M data.
- **Qwen3-1.7B is the lite fallback, not the agent.** Its tool calling is weaker (v4 28.41, multi-turn 11.0), and its quality gap to the 4B is −0.111 on the claim kit. That gap is mostly knowledge, shrinking to −0.032 when the fact chunk is present, so pack retrieval matters more for the lite tier than a reader swap.
- **Qwen3.6-35B-A3B is the strongest tool model measured (BFCLv4 57.83 FC with thinking, TAU3 67.2, MCP-Atlas 62.8) and our best quality tier (V-para 0.436 floor-adj).** It needs a 12 GB phone and takes about 2–3 min per answer on a Pixel 8 Pro, and bmoe has no thinking budget. llama.cpp b11010 does have `--reasoning-budget`, so a deep agentic tier is a later, device-gated step.
- **MiniCPM5-1B is not a tool-calling option.** Its BFCL v4 is 25.15 (thinking), about Qwen3-0.6B level. Our replay showed no quality gain and showed runaway and empty answers. Its value is abstention discipline only.
