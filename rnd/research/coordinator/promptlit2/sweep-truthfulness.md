## Gaps

1. No measured small-model (<=8B) A/B of an abstention/payoff/confidence system prompt on factual QA with an over-refusal column in the same run. Closest: row 1 (refusal side-effect measured, but no prompt tested) and row 4 (prompt given, numbers not read).
2. Chain-of-verification / answer-then-verify at 3B-8B: not found as a prompt-only, measured study. CoVe's original numbers use 65B models.
3. Answer length vs factual precision at small sizes: no 2024-2026 small-model source found beyond the excluded Zhao paper.
4. Liquid AI LFM2 / LFM2.5: no official system-prompt or factuality guidance found. Third-party listing (Together AI) says LFM2.5-8B-A1B has abstention training; AA-Omniscience scores negative. Not a source, a lead. Read the LFM2 technical report (arXiv 2511.23404) next.
5. Gemma-3 and Phi-4-mini SimpleQA / hallucination numbers: aggregator sites disagree (0 % to 4 %) and state no settings. Need the Gemma 3 report (arXiv 2503.19786) and the Phi-4-mini report, eval tables only.
6. Qwen3 small-model SimpleQA thinking vs non-thinking with abstention: not found (MARGO gives TriviaQA only). Zhao 2509.06861 Table 3 is the place to look (cut off in fetch; re-fetch with offset 100000 or the PDF).
7. Verbalized confidence AUROC for Llama-3.2-3B / Qwen2.5 small models: row 1 gives Llama-3.2-3B 0.52, but only on pooled TriviaQA + Global-MMLU. Need per-dataset numbers.
8. Do the two 2026 papers (rows 1, 8) hold for prompted (not finetuned) confidence? Unclear from abstracts.
9. The Gemma/Phi/LFM vendor prompting guides (Qwen, Liquid, Google, Microsoft, Meta) did not come back as text in these searches; not checked directly.
10. Row 2 numbers come from the v1 HTML; v3 (AAAI camera-ready) may differ. Re-check before quoting.

Caveats for use: numbers are not comparable across rows (different benchmarks, judges, and refusal definitions). Row 1 ECE values conflict between text and table. Table 1 values in row 2 are Brier scores, not error rates.
