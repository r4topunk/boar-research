# Open-weight READERS for RAG QA: literature sweep (2026-10-13)

Scope: which open-weight LLMs read a provided passage faithfully. Class A = fast path ≤ ~5 GB GGUF (3–9B). Class B = deep tier ≤ 13 GB GGUF (≤ 35B MoE).
Read-only web sweep, no downloads. Confidence: VERIFIED = number read on the primary page (via WebFetch); UNVERIFIED = search snippet or secondary summary.

## TL;DR
- No 2026 benchmark isolates grounded QA reading for small models. Closest live proxy: Vectara hallucination leaderboard (summarisation grounding, last updated 2026-09-22 per fetch). Not a QA reader test.
- On that proxy, Phi-4 14B (3.7 % HR) and Qwen3-8B (4.8 %) lead the open models that fit a local tier. Phi-4-mini (23.5 %) and Ministral-3 3B (24.2 %) are weak.
- Within the Qwen3 family size barely moves the rate (4B 5.7, 8B 4.8, 14B 5.4, 32B 5.9): training family matters more than size here.
- Granite 4.x and Command R7B are marketed for RAG/citation, but no independent number was found. Treat as UNVERIFIED.
- Only one 2026 paper ranks small GGUF readers on a RAG-faithfulness metric (Russian, LLM-judge): Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 0.80 vs Qwen3-8B 0.83 (secondary summary).
- Gap: no published reader number exists for Qwen3.5-4B, Gemma 4 E2B/E4B, or SmolLM3 in this sweep.

## Candidates for a local reader sweep (GGUF ≤ 13 GB, ranked)

1. Phi-4 14B (B): Vectara HR 3.7 %, best fitting open model. Verify GGUF size at Q4_K_M (≈ 8–9 GB expected).
2. Qwen3-8B (A/edge): HR 4.8 %; reader faithfulness 0.83 on Russian RAG. Quant to ≤ 5 GB.
3. Gemma 3 12B (B): HR 4.4 %. Q4 ≈ 7 GB (UNVERIFIED).
4. Qwen3-14B (B): HR 5.4 %; same family as baseline, direct upgrade test.
5. Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 (A, baseline): HR 5.7 %; faithfulness 0.80.
6. Gemma 3 4B (A): HR 6.4 %.
7. Gemma 4 26B-A4B (B only at ~Q3): HR 5.2 %; ≈ 15 GB at Q4 so likely excluded by the 13 GB cap.
8. Ministral-3-8B or Granite 3.3-8B: skip for now (HR 21.7 % / 10.6 %). Keep only if the licence is needed.

## Gaps

- No 2026 benchmark measures reader faithfulness (QA with a supplied passage) for ≤ 9B models on a common set.
- Vectara HR is a summarisation task with a hosted judge: it is a proxy, not our QA target.
- Gemma 4 E2B/E4B, Qwen3.5 small, SmolLM3, Granite 4.x RAG-specific numbers: not found.
- Command R7B and Nemotron-Nano-4B: RAG training claimed, no independent number.
- The Russian RAG study numbers are from a secondary summary; the PDF text was not readable. Check the paper tables before citing.
- Licences: Command R7B (CC-BY-NC) and Nemotron-Mini (community licence) need a check before any distribution.
- GGUF sizes above are estimates unless marked VERIFIED; confirm on HF before a sweep.
