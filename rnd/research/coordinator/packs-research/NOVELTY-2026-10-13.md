# Is the BOAR pack-stack measurement already published? Novelty check (2026-10-13)

TL;DR: **not frontier, but a real gap.** An 8-agent search (6 haiku sweeps, one per angle, ≈ 60 findings; 2 opus verifiers reading the primary pages of every candidate flagged as prior work) found **no paper that measures what BOAR measures**: a ≤ 9B reader on phone-class hardware, several heterogeneous offline packs, routing among them, the reach → keep → reader funnel, under a frozen paired criterion. Two 2026 preprints overlap PARTIALLY and must be cited: Pandey 2603.11513 (oracle-utilisation ceiling of sub-8B readers) and Bala 2607.00725 ("answer-in-context" = our keep stage). Every mechanism we use is prior art; the **combination and the measurement discipline** are what is new. Status of the gap claim: supported by one serious pass; a report should word it as "to our knowledge" and cite the two partials.

Sweeps: Semantic Scholar/arXiv/ACL/OpenReview/HF papers/GitHub via WebSearch+WebFetch, ≥ 8 queries per angle. ## 2. Must-cite list for the technical report
1. Pandey, "Can Small Language Models Use What They Retrieve?" arXiv 2603.11513 (2026, under ARR review). Known/Unknown split + gold-passage oracle = our parametric-vs-reader decomposition; paired McNemar. Our DEEPREAD/ORACLE-ST replicate its reading on domain packs and extend it to a 35B MoE.
2. Bala, "Recall Is Not Enough: A Reader-Context Diagnostic for Budget-Constrained Retrieval" arXiv 2607.00725 (2026, under EACL review). Answer-in-context = our keep metric; separates retrieval failure from packing failure. Our funnel adds the pack-crowding dimension.
3. Joren et al., "Sufficient Context" ICLR 2025 (2411.06037): retrieval-vs-reader failure autorater.
4. Zero-RAG 2511.00505 and Less LLM More Documents 2510.02657 (ECIR 2026): the two sides of the corpus-size question.
5. Levy et al. EMNLP 2025 Findings and Amiraz et al. ACL 2025 (crowding, distractors), already in LIT-PACKS.md; Yoran ICLR 2024; Liu "Lost in the Middle" TACL 2024; Mallen ACL 2023 (when retrieval helps vs parametric).
6. RAGRoute 2502.19280 and Adaptive-RAG NAACL 2024 (routing baselines); RouterRetriever AAAI 2025 (in LIT-PACKS).
7. Pocket RAG 2602.13229, MoRAGBench, SmartRAG 2607.14661 (on-device neighbours).
8. Judge methodology: Zheng et al. NeurIPS 2023; Judging the Judges AACL 2025.

## 3. How to word it
- Say: "To our knowledge no prior work measures, for one small offline reader on phone-class hardware, domain packs against a general corpus, routing among installed packs, and the reach/keep/reader split under pre-registered paired criteria. The closest are [1] and [2], which we extend to heterogeneous local corpora and to a 35B MoE tier."
- Do not say: "first", "novel method", "frontier". Every component (BM25+dense hybrid, routing, oracle ceilings, doc2query, LLM judge with swaps) is prior art.
- The contribution is a **measurement study with a public dataset** (9 packs + 180 self-tests on HF) and clean negatives, not a new technique.

## 4. Limits of this check
- One pass, abstract-level for most sweeps; two papers read in full text. Semantic Scholar was not directly reachable; Google Scholar via WebSearch is lossy.
- Candidates verified = only those a haiku sweep flagged as prior work (2). Non-flagged findings were not re-read by opus.
- Search in English only; the Russian 2026 CPU-RAG study suggests non-English venues hold more on-device work.
- Repeat the two-paper verification before the report is published, and re-run the sweep the week of publication (new preprints appear weekly in this area: 4 of the closest papers are from 2026).
