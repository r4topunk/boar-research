# Literature pointers for the Knowledge Pack line (2026-10-13; web, read-only)
- Crowding / many documents: Levy et al., "More Documents, Same Length: Isolating the Challenge of Multiple Documents in RAG" (EMNLP 2025 Findings) — at fixed context length, more retrieved documents cost up to −20 % for most LLMs (Qwen2 an exception). https://preview.aclanthology.org/dashboard/2025.findings-emnlp.1064
- Distractors: Amiraz et al., "The Distracting Effect: Understanding Irrelevant Passages in RAG" (ACL 2025) — related-but-wrong passages are the harmful class; fine-tuning with selected distractors +7.5 %. https://arxiv.org/html/2505.06914v1
- Noise debate: Cuconasu et al., "The Power of Noise" (2024; random documents can help) https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/2401.14887v3 vs "Toward Optimal Search and Retrieval for RAG" (2024; injected noise degrades; nothing beats the gold ceiling) https://arxiv.org/html/2411.07396v1
- Routing to one expert: Lee et al., "RouterRetriever: Routing over a Mixture of Expert Embedding Models" (AAAI 2025) — per-query routing to a domain expert beats a general retriever (+2.1 nDCG@10 on BEIR) and multi-task training; oracle routing still better. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.02685
- Resource selection (federated search): CORI, ReDDE; survey "Federated search techniques: an overview of the trends and state of the art" (KAIS 2023) https://pure.ulster.ac.uk/en/publications/federated-search-techniques-an-overview-of-the-trends-and-state-o/ ; Nguyen et al. 2016 web-scale resource selection https://arxiv.org/pdf/1609.04556 — PACKROUTE = resource selection over installed packs.
- Small models + domain corpora offline: "Pocket RAG: On-Device RAG for First Aid Guidance in Offline Mobile Environment" (2026; 0.6–1B models; RAG + rerank help, the base model sets the ceiling) https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.13229 ; "Improving the Domain Adaptation of RAG Models for ODQA" (TACL) — Wikipedia-pretrained retrievers favour Wikipedia-like KBs https://direct.mit.edu/tacl/article/doi/10.1162/tacl_a_00530/114590/Improving-the-Domain-Adaptation-of-Retrieval
- Gap: no study compares, for one small offline model, a domain KB vs a general Wikipedia corpus, nor routing among installed packs — SELFTEST-SCORES (with/without pack, 180 items) + PACKROUTE are that measurement.
How these map to our numbers: simplewiki as a peer takes 0.83 of kept slots and displaces gold (Levy/Amiraz); with-pack lift tracks kept-from-pack share (reach, not size); the 4B converts little of the reached facts (Pocket RAG: base model ceiling).
