## 2. Training-side evidence for H10 SFT4B
- **RAFT, LLaMA2-7B** (2403.10131) [V]: PubMed 73.3 vs DSF+RAG 71.6 vs base+RAG 58.8; HotpotQA 35.28 vs base+RAG 0.03. **[C]** the quoted "+35 %" starts from a format failure at 0.03; the like-for-like gain for a well-formatted instruct reader is PubMed's ≈ +2 over domain fine-tuning with RAG. CoT +9.66 on HotpotQA; gold document in P ≈ 80 % of training examples helps. https://arxiv.org/html/2403.10131v2
- **RAG-Instruct** (EMNLP 2025, Tables 4/7) [V; EM/accuracy, not faithfulness]: Qwen2.5-7B base + 40K synthetic RAG instructions avg 64.5 vs Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct 58.4 (+6.1), PubMed 72.7 vs 62.6; Llama-3.1-8B 68.6 vs 60.0. **Warning for SFT4B:** fine-tuning on SQuAD2.0 (130K short extractive answers) gives 54.7 = −5.3 vs the instruct model. https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.192.pdf
- **Ext2Gen** (WSDM 2026; up to 25 distractor chunks; GPT-4o judge) [V]: Qwen2.5-3B default 0.516 → best SFT 0.609 → Ext2Gen 0.649 vs 0.877 with gold chunks only; Llama-3.2-3B 0.595 → 0.649 → 0.750. https://arxiv.org/html/2503.04789v3
- **CANOE** (2505.16483; RL on GPT-4o synthetic data) [V; some cells ambiguous]: avg accuracy Qwen2.5-7B 60.2 → 72.4, Llama-3-8B 57.4 → 73.6; realistic RAG-QA (FollowRAG) only 45.3 → 50.2 / 44.8 → 51.7; CLAPNQ faithfulness +4 to +6. https://arxiv.org/html/2505.16483v3
- **Trust-Align** (ICLR 2025): 26/27 models improve incl. Qwen2.5 0.5–7B, Phi-3.5 [V abstract]; correctness trade-off [U]. https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.11242
- **CRAFT-RL** (2602.01348): accuracy and faithfulness rise from 1.5B upward; at 0.5B depends on the template [V abstract]. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01348
- **Strict rubric:** up to 57 % of RAG citations are unfaithful / post-rationalised (Wallat 2412.18004 [V], https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.18004). **No paper reports SFT gains under a per-claim rubric as strict as our ≤ 2/20 unsupported bar [U];** CANOE's CLAPNQ faithfulness is the closest.

## 3. Prompt-side levers (model unchanged)
**What the engine already does** (kiln-local/be-0cfd4a77-cut7, 0cfd4a77): sources numbered `[n] title\nbody`, most relevant first (`src/rag/compress.ts:152`); sources in the system message, question last (`src/rag/pure.ts:378-397`) = "query after documents"; soft citation request with permissive fallback (`pure.ts:331-333`: "Use the context below when relevant, and cite a source you used by its number… If the context doesn't cover the question, say so and answer from general knowledge") → no "answer only from the passage" framing, by design; extractive compression to 1,200 tokens, ≤ 4 sentences per chunk, chunk dropped under half the best score (`compress.ts:177-184`); post-hoc removal of unsupported `[n]` (CT-1, `src/routing/answer.ts:1413-1417`), uncited warning `answer.ts:925`; extractive instant tier (`answer.ts:1076-1110`), health answers = source text (`answer.ts:1126`); succinct persona 2–3 sentences (`src/constants/personalities.ts:41-46`). **Not done:** model-written quote-then-answer, CoT on the 4B, alternative passage orderings.

**Literature:**
- Prompt templates on small readers (2602.13890, Feb 2026; Qwen2.5-3B, Gemma3-4B; HotpotQA 18,720; GPT-4o-mini judge; no CIs) [V PDF]: standard Qwen 0.768 / Gemma 0.785; hierarchical_synthesis 0.788 (+2.0) / 0.843 (+5.8) at ×1.37 / ×1.7 time; chain_of_thought 0.773 (+0.5) / 0.831 (+4.6) at ×2.6 / ×3.1; role_playing 0.754 (−1.4) / 0.833; best hybrids up to +6 at ×8–10. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13890
- Passage ordering (SIGIR 2026 reproduction, 2605.27105): no clear U-shape with modern LLMs; Gemma-3-4B reversed order "small positive" F1 [V]; with 6 passages ≤ 1,200 tokens expect ≈ 0. https://arxiv.org/html/2605.27105
- Quote-first: LLMQuoter (2501.05554) 3B reader 24.4 % → 62.2 % only with gold quotes, quoter F1 69.1 [U]; "According to…" (2305.13252) small models same or worse [U]; Pleias-RAG (2504.18225) native quote grounding, no numbers in the abstract [V]. Device cost: the 4B decodes 2.0–2.7 tok/s on the A15 → a 25-token quote ≈ +10 s.
- Strict "answer only from the passage": no 2025–2026 measurement on ≤ 4B found [U]; for it: Pandey's dominant failure is ignoring context; against: the 4B scores 0.460 without the pack (SELFTEST), so strict framing risks losing closed-book answers.
