## Corrections that change the plan

- **First-aid source:** build from **ATP 4-02.11 (Mar 2026)**, not TC 4-02.1. It supersedes TC 4-02.1, is public release, and is 12.7 MB on archive.org. FM 4-25.11 (2002) is two generations old: keep it only as a fallback.
- **SE archive.org data is the 2024-04-02 snapshot**, about 2.5 years stale. Anonymous download works today. For fresher content, convert Kiwix 2026 ZIMs via python-libzim (private `_get_entry_by_id` iteration plus our own HTML stripping), or accept the staleness.
- **SE attribution is per post**: author name and profile link for every Q and A, plus a link to the question. The pack schema needs `author`, `author_url` and `question_url` per row, not only `url` and `license` (`article_meta` today).
- **The licence varies per post (2.5/3.0/4.0 by date)**, so store the creation date or the licence per row. SE packs are BY-SA, so derived packs are share-alike.
- **MedlinePlus XML is shippable as is**: `full-summary` is PD NLM text; A.D.A.M. appears only as links. Keep the credit line "Courtesy of MedlinePlus from the National Library of Medicine". Do not inline any ency/ or drug pages.
- **NHS is a poor fit for an offline pack**: OGL copies must be refreshed ≥ 7 days or stamped "as at DDMMYY". No charge may be levied for access, which conflicts with any paid tier. The API needs registration and a legal basis. Prefer the Kiwix `nhs.uk_en_medicines` ZIM only after reading its terms. Otherwise skip NHS.
- **WikiMed is current**: wikipedia_en_medicine 2026-10, nopic 1.0 GB / mini 166 MB. The nopic ZIM is a ready alternative to filtering the enwiki dump ourselves.
- FDC numbers: use the 04/2026 release (460 MB zip), not Dec 2025.
