# gap_device_access: getting a 12 GB Snapdragon/Tensor phone into the measurement loop without owning one

As of 2026-10-12. Read-only web research. No accounts were created, nothing was downloaded, and no ToS was accepted. Every service below needs an account action, and those belong to r4to.
Scope: remote real-device services (APK sideload plus a long-lived native process), sustained thermal data for 8 Elite / 8 Elite Gen 5 / Tensor G4/G5, and used-phone price bands (a list, not purchase advice).
Extends engine_speed.md (thermal row: a competitor team G3 3rd-request −[competitor score removed] decode) and prior_art.md (bmoe PR #219 on a 12 GB Hexagon v81 phone). Neither note checked rented devices; the critique flagged this as the largest blocker ("no device").

## TL;DR
1. **Google's Developer Device Platform (DDP, public preview since 2026-08-12) and Android Studio Device Streaming give real Pixels over full "ADB over SSL".** They list Pixel 9 / 9 Pro / 9 Pro Fold. The DDP agent-skill docs use "Get a Pixel 10" as an example, and Pixel 9 and Pixel 10 are 12 GB Tensor G4/G5 devices. Partner labs add Samsung, OnePlus, Xiaomi and others. Preview price: [usage detail removed] per device-minute for streaming and [usage detail removed] for Device Run. Sessions start with a 15-min TTL that can be extended; no documented cap. This is the cleanest path to a **12 GB Tensor** measurement. https://developer.android.com/studio/run/android-device-streaming · https://docs.cloud.google.com/developer-device-platform/reference/device-streaming/rest/v1/projects.deviceSessions · https://cloud.google.com/products/developer-device-platform?hl=zh-tw
2. **Qualcomm Device Cloud (QDC) is free and already runs llama.cpp CPU/GPU/NPU benches over adb on SM8750 (8 Elite) and SM8850 (8 Elite Gen 5) reference phones.** llama.cpp's own CI does this: it pushes binaries to /data/local/tmp, then `curl`s the GGUF **on the device** from Hugging Face, with a 3,600 s job timeout and a 90-min workflow timeout. Caveat: the reference designs are press-reported at **24 GB RAM**, so this is not a 12 GB proxy without a self-imposed memory cap. https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/scripts/snapdragon/qdc/tests/run_bench_tests_posix.py · https://qdc.qualcomm.com/support/faq
3. **Samsung Remote Test Lab is free and has adb through the Remote Debug Bridge (RDB).** It lists the Galaxy S26 Ultra (SM-S948), whose base SKU is 12 GB and uses the 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy. Each user gets 20 credits a day at 15 min per credit (≈ 5 h/day); the documented maximum reservation is 10 h/day. Pushing a 12.3 GB GGUF through RDB over the internet is the open risk: no speed or size limit is documented. https://developer.samsung.com/remotetestlab/doc/about-remote-test-lab · https://www.gsmarena.com/samsung_galaxy_s26_ultra-review-2939p4.php
4. **AWS Device Farm custom test environment:** the test host has adb (platform-tools) and internet access, and arbitrary shell commands run in install/pre_test/test phases. There is a **150-min hard cap** per run and per remote-access session, and a 4 GB app limit. Price is [usage detail removed] per device-minute; the one-time trial is **1,000 free minutes**; unmetered is [usage detail removed]/slot/month. Remote access sessions do **not** give you your own adb (re:Post); you only get adb inside the testspec. https://docs.aws.amazon.com/devicefarm/latest/developerguide/limits.html · https://aws.amazon.com/device-farm/pricing/
5. **These do not fit (cannot run a native binary or a 12 GB model):**
   - BrowserStack: adb allowlist only (setprop/dumpsys/getprop/settings), 1 GB app limit, 2 h sessions.
   - LambdaTest: allowlisted `lambda-adb`.
   - Qualcomm AI Hub profile jobs: compiled QNN/TFLite/ONNX models only, no GGUF or custom binaries.
   - Genymotion: emulators.
   - Firebase Test Lab: instrumentation only, 45-min physical cap, **deprecated, shuts down 2027-09-30** (DDP replaces it).
   - Sauce Labs has an adb-shell API, but on Enterprise plans only.

   https://www.browserstack.com/docs/app-live/adb-commands · https://firebase.google. [usage detail removed]
6. **Thermal: no public multi-minute llama.cpp CPU-decode trace exists for 8 Elite / 8 Elite Gen 5 / Tensor G4/G5.** Proxies:
   - GSMArena: the S26 Ultra CPU sustains **< 50 % of peak** under stress, with no gain over the S25 Ultra.
   - Pixel 9 Pro XL (Tensor G4): CPU throttles after about 3 min, bottoms near 50 % at about 4 min, and steadies near 65 % (secondary, via an X post).
   - Galaxy S24 Ultra (8 Gen 3), MLC GPU, 1.5B: plateau −15 % (min −22 %) over 20 iterations.

   The only multi-request LLM thermal data for our exact stack is a competitor team's Tensor G3 (−[competitor score removed] decode on the 3rd request; known). https://arxiv.org/html/2603.23640v2
7. **Engine-level warning for Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5:** MNN reports CPU decode collapsing to 1.6 tok/s on two S26 Ultras, against 8.9 tok/s on an S25 Ultra with the same build. That is MNN, not llama.cpp, but it is a reason to measure CPU decode on an SM8850 before trusting any plan number. https://github.com/alibaba/MNN/issues/4922
8. **Used-price bands (snapshots, dates partly unknown):**
   - Pixel 8 Pro (12 GB, G3), US: [usage detail removed]–400.
   - Galaxy S24 Ultra (12 GB, 8 Gen 3), BR (OLX): R[usage detail removed]–4,200.

## Q1. Remote real-device services: 12 GB models, adb or native process, session, storage, model push, price

How a 12.3 GB GGUF gets onto the device. Three patterns exist, in order of preference:
- **(a) Device-side download.** `adb shell curl` or the bench app's own downloader pulls the file onto the device from HF/S3. The QDC llama.cpp CI uses exactly this.
- **(b) Host-side `adb push` from a cloud host that sits next to the device.** This is Device Farm's testspec host, which has internet access.
- **(c) `adb push` from r4to's Mac over an ADB-over-SSL/RDB tunnel.** This is bounded by home uplink plus tunnel speed, which is unknown. At 10 MB/s it would take ≈ 20 min; at 2 MB/s, ≈ 1.7 h.

Per-device reservations are wiped after each session (DDP, QDC, RTL), so the model has to be re-fetched every session.
