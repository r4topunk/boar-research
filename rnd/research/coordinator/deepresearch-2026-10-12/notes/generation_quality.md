# generation_quality: inference-time methods for long-form factuality and completeness on 4–35B readers (wrong-fact and thin buckets)

State as of 2026-10-12. Researcher: deep-research sweep (Opus). Builds on `websweep-2026-10-06/small-reader-reasoning.md` (CoVe, rubric RL, CURE, HalluGuard, ASC, sub-question retrieval, TREC nuggets: all `known`, not repeated here) and `LIT-GROUNDED-READING-2026-10-06.md` (RioRAG, R-Tuning: `known`). Numbers marked "verified" were read in the paper PDF (pdftotext) or repo API; "abstract" = abstract/search-snippet level only.
Local check (verified, 2026-10-12): `boar-app/node_modules/llama.rn` 0.13.0-rc.7 exposes per-token probabilities (`n_probs`, `rn-completion.cpp`) and slot-based parallel completions (`context.parallel`, README l.506), but has **no CFG / two-context logit combination** (no `apply_guidance`/`cfg_scale` in its cpp sources). llama.cpp had CFG via a second context (`--cfg-negative-prompt`, commit #4951, Jan 2024); it is absent from the llama.rn tree today. Any CAD / logit-merge method therefore needs a native patch (custom decode loop over `llama_get_logits_ith` for several sequences). bmoe multi-sequence decode: UNKNOWN.

## TL;DR
- **Layer-contrast decoding (DoLa, SLED, END, ActLCD) is closed for Qwen-family instruct models.** DoLa on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct cuts FActScore 40.7 → 28.6; SLED 39.1, END 38.3 (Table 1 of [DescaPE 2609.15745](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15745)); in [Integrative Decoding 2410.01556](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.01556) DoLa is within ±1.3 of greedy on all six 7–9B models. Do not test.
- **Context-faithfulness decoding (CAD → AdaCAD → CoCoA) helps only where a source was kept, and fixed CAD hurts.** CoCoA on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct LFQA: avg faithfulness 52.59 → 57.74 (+5.2; CAD +2.1, AdaCAD +3.3); CAD on Llama3-8B short QA averages 49.08 vs greedy 60.38 (−11.3) ([CoCoA 2508.17670](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17670), EMNLP 2025, MIT code). BOAR's dominant loss (no source kept on 29/51 V-para) is out of its reach; needs a 2-sequence native loop.
- **The only decoding-time family with closed-book long-form gains on both precision AND recall at 3–32B is sample-ensembling at the logit level.** Integrative Decoding (ICLR 2025): LongFact F1@128 +3.4…+8.5 on six 7–9B models (Qwen2-7B 69.1 → 74.8, recall 57.1 → 64.2), Biographies accuracy +3.3 (Qwen2.5-3B) / +8.1 (7B) / +10.5 (32B); text-level ensembles (USC, FSC, Self-Reflection) *lose* 3–25 F1 on LongFact ([2410.01556](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.01556)). ThinkMerge (ICLR 2026) does the same over K parallel thinking traces at the answer step: Qwen3-4B AIME 68.0 → 72.0 at K = 4 vs majority vote; WebSailor deep-research +4.8 GAIA / +10.2 xbench ([2512.02874](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02874)). This is a **new mechanism** vs the closed best-of-2 / self-selection family (DEEPBO2): merging logits, not picking a sample. It fits SEEDVAR's finding (per-item seed r 0.5–0.7, oracle best-of-2 +0.038).
- **Cost is the blocker for the phone:** ID needs k sampled responses (k = 4–16) plus a k-way batched pass; ThinkMerge needs K thinking traces. On a memory-bound CPU, batched decode of a dense 4B is cheaper than K× serial, but for the expert-streamed 35B different sequences touch different experts (UNKNOWN multiplier). Desktop measurement is cheap; device viability is not shown.
- **Token-probability claim signals are weak at this scale.** CCP claim-level ROC-AUC 0.56–0.68 on 6–13B models vs max-prob 0.52–0.63 ([2403.04696](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.04696), abstract/table snippet); sub-3B token entropy ≈ 0 on 91 % of dataset–model pairs ([2609.20824](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20824), MCQ only). llama.rn already exposes `n_probs`, so a logprob-based "drop/flag low-confidence sentence" pass is free to build but unlikely to clear MDE.
- **Hidden-state probes are the only cheap pre-generation knowledge signal in the literature.** Semantic Entropy Probes predict semantic entropy from a single forward pass, even from the token *before* generation, on Llama-2-7B / Mistral-7B / Phi-3-3.8B / 70B ([2406.15927](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.15927)). This is a new mechanism for the closed routing family (reopen rule: a pre-generation signal capturing ≥ 70 % of the deep gain at ≤ 40 % deep), and an abstain trigger for absent-knowledge items. llama.cpp can read intermediate tensors via its eval callback; not exposed in llama.rn JS.
- **Answer planning / nugget pipelines and agentic retrieval loops are closed families here.** Crucible / GINGER nugget banks are multi-call pipelines on Llama-3.3-70B / GPT-4 class ([2601.13222](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13222)); our prompt-level key-point plan (DEEPCOVER) lost −0.027 with correctness −0.11; Search-o1 / ReSearch / R1-Searcher are iterative retrieval (DEEPITER closed) and cannot fetch facts that WIKISECTION showed are not in the kit.
- **Abstention that keeps coverage needs training (TruthRL ternary reward: hallucination 43.5 % → 19.4 %, truthfulness 5.3 % → 37.2 %, [2509.25760](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25760)) or a calibrated post-hoc filter (conformal back-off: 80–90 % factuality guarantees while keeping most claims on GPT-4, [2402.10978](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10978)).** Whether converting *wrong* items to abstentions raises BOAR's floor-adjusted number depends on how judge.v1 scores a wrong answer vs a decline (UNKNOWN; 0-call check proposed below).

## Q3. Abstention and calibration for small models

Takeaway: verbalized and token-level confidence of 4–8B models is near chance for claims; sampling-based (semantic entropy, LUQ) signals work but cost k generations; single-pass hidden-state probes (SEP) are the cheap option and also give a pre-generation signal. Abstention that preserves coverage is a training result (TruthRL), not an inference trick.

Retrieval-in-the-loop agents are closed here (DEEPITER) and cannot add facts the kit does not hold.
