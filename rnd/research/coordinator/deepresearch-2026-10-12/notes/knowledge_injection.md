# Knowledge injection: putting missing facts into weights or memory instead of the prompt (absent-knowledge bucket)

Researcher angle: `knowledge_injection`. Date: 2026-10-12. Method: ~25 WebSearch/WebFetch calls, GitHub REST and HF Hub API for licences and sizes, one PDF read in full (Active Reading). Builds on `websweep-2026-10-06/precomputed-knowledge.md` (PAQ/RePAQ, EntiGraph, GenRead, imitation and new-knowledge fine-tuning papers) and `websweep-2026-10-06/small-model-knowledge.md` (Engram, SCONE, MoLE, Memory Layers, TF-Engram, Memory Grafting). Those items are cited, not re-derived.

## TL;DR

- **The binding constraint is the source, not the mechanism.** Every injection method found (PRAG/DyPRAG, Knowledge Modules, Cartridges, synthetic CPT, Active Reading, editing) moves text you already hold into weights or KV. WIKISECTION (10-12) says 12/13 absent facts are not in our enwiki pack, so injecting our own corpora cannot add them. The only sources holding them are a frontier model (paid API, needs r4to's OK) or a not-yet-identified non-Wikipedia corpus. Weight injection from our own 35B adds nothing the deep tier lacks. (Inference; see [Active Reading](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.09494), its gains require the source documents.)
- **For a 1–8B model, parametric injection is at best on par with RAG and only adds a few points when combined with it.** Reassessment (Jan 2026): P-RAG beats text RAG only on Qwen2.5-7B (33.26 vs 31.33 HasAnswer), loses on 1–1.5B; hybrid best (+2.6 to +3.1 over RAG) — [arXiv 2510.12668](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.12668). Knowledge Modules on Llama-3.1-8B: KM 39.0 vs RAG 41.0 on QuALITY; RAG+KM 41.1 — [arXiv 2503.08727](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.08727v3).
- **Synthetic CPT works well when the source documents exist, but at a data/compute scale we cannot reach locally.** Active Reading: Llama-3.1-8B on SimpleWikiQA 7.42 → 66.25 (= gold-in-context 65.85) using ~4 B generated words and 20k steps × 128 × 4096 tokens (~10 B tokens, full fine-tune); recall falls as the document pool grows — [arXiv 2508.09494](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.09494). SPA (2026): simple 7-prompt augmentation ties or beats EntiGraph/Active Reading at 350–4000× corpus size — [arXiv 2603.22213](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.22213v2).
- **Meta WikiExpert-8B (released) is the strongest small closed-book fact model found: SimpleQA 23.5 vs 7.3 for Llama-3.1-8B (Llama 405B 17.1).** But it is a Wikipedia expert (our absent facts are outside Wikipedia), a base model with lower GSM8k (48.6 vs 54.4) and MBPP (39.4 vs 48.0), and **CC-BY-NC-4.0** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.09494), [HF card](https://huggingface.co/facebook/meta-wiki-expert).
- **Knowledge editing does not scale to long-form use.** MEMIT collapses after ~250 sequential edits in small batches (WikiBigEdit), editing loses to LoRA-FT and LoRA-merge after ~100k edits, RAG ~3× accuracy on edits — [arXiv 2503.05683](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.05683); AlphaEdit degrades past ~3–5k edits — [arXiv 2606.26783](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26783). UltraEdit claims 2M edits at ~80 % efficacy, but these are single-triple probes and general ability was not checked past 20k edits — [arXiv 2505.14679](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.14679v3).
- **A LoRA learns only a few hundred new facts reliably.** Llama-3.1-8B: 500 unknown facts at 100 % reliability; 3,000 not learned in 10 epochs; external QA benchmarks decline — [arXiv 2502.14502](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.14502v2). 4-bit PTQ costs 2.5–5.8 pt of fact recall at 7–8B, and the loss is larger on relations that are not saturated — [arXiv 2505.13963](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.13963v3).
- **PAQ (65M QA pairs) closes no absent-knowledge gap for us.** It is generated from Wikipedia. Its data licence is CC-BY-SA but its code is **CC-BY-NC**, and the recommended RePAQ index is 16 GB (flat SQ8, 16 GB RAM) — [GitHub](https://github.com/facebookresearch/PAQ). The question-shaped index idea survives only as a delivery format for a frontier-written store (O6 in the 10-06 review).
- **On-device plumbing exists for one mechanism only:** llama.rn exposes `applyLoraAdapters` / `removeLoraAdapters` (checked in `boar-app/node_modules/llama.rn/src/index.ts` l.1102). Per-topic LoRA hot-swap (PRAG-style) is therefore technically possible. Logit interpolation (Memory Decoder, kNN-LM), embedding-token input (xRAG) and trained KV prefixes (Cartridges) all need native work.

- A published long-form (rubric-judged, 150–300 word answer) evaluation of any parametric injection method (PRAG, KM, editing, CPT): none; all use EM/F1/MCQ on short answers.
