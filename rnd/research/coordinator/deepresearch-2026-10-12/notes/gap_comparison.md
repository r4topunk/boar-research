# gap_comparison: comparison/synthesis answers from small/MoE readers + open per-entity attribute tables

State as of 2026-10-12. Read-only sweep: about 32 tool calls (WebSearch/WebFetch, Databus SPARQL, GitHub/HF APIs, HEAD requests for sizes). Nothing over 100 MB was downloaded, and blind/ and veil/ were not touched. Builds on generation_quality.md Q5 (MEBench, DER-RAG, table-first, all relevance ≤ 1 there), structured_knowledge.md (KG-Infused RAG, WDGATE, Wikidata5M), datastores.md (Factbook, SE), arms/synthdeep/SYNTHDEEP.md (cmpsplit closed at step 0: both sides kept on 1→2/7 curated, 2→5/7 with wiki-vital5; 4/7 comparison items lose primarily on *wrong* facts).

## TL;DR
- **The small-vs-large gap on comparisons is knowledge/extraction, not structure.** On MEBench comparative questions, Llama-3-8B + RAG scores 0.447 vs GPT-4 + RAG 0.763 (ratio 0.59). Closed-book it is 0.046 vs 0.199. An 8B fine-tuned for extraction + RAG reaches 0.687. The paper names "fail to locate all entity and their attributes" as the failure mode. https://arxiv.org/html/2502.18993v3 (Table 3)
- **On a 19-point structure rubric for comparative answers, an 8B is close to GPT-4.** Llama-3-8B scores 16.58 vs GPT-4 17.77 under the GPT-4 evaluator, and 14.77 vs 15.96 under human evaluation (Touché-2022 topics). Structured prompts (aspects, then an explicit choice) rank highest. Adding retrieved CAM arguments did *not* help (scenario 3 > 4). → The format is not where BOAR's 0.47 cell loses. This agrees with SYNTHDEEP ("wrong facts about the sides"). https://arxiv.org/html/2502.14476v1 (Table 2)
- **StructRAG ("restructure the evidence into a table, then answer") is the strongest measured table-first result.** Loong *Comparison* LLM-score goes 44.5 → 75.6 (10–50k-token doc sets) vs plain RAG. But it uses a Qwen2-72B structurizer/utilizer, a DPO-trained Qwen2-7B router, and 9.7 min end-to-end per set on GPUs. It applies only when the evidence for both sides is already in hand, and BOAR has that on ≤ 2/7 (curated) comparison items. https://arxiv.org/html/2410.08815v2
- **Infobox/table sources add little over text even at large scale.** On CompMix, SPAGHETTI (GPT-3.5) goes text-only 53.8 EM → text + tables/infoboxes 55.6 (+1.8 EM, +3.4 GPT-4-match), and the KB adds +2.9 more. https://arxiv.org/html/2406.00562v1 (Table 2). **BOAR's wiki builder already writes each article's infobox "Key facts" (≤ 800 chars) as text** (rnd/research/sonar/cards/full-enwiki-pack-arm.md l.68), so "add infoboxes" is mostly already in the kit. Only an *aligned two-entity* card is new.
- **Fresh structured infoboxes need no download.** FineWiki, our current source (Aug 2025 Enterprise HTML), ships an `infoboxes` JSON column (key–value) per article. https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/finewiki. DBpedia's official English infobox dumps stopped at **2022-12-01** (mappingbased-literals 152.9 MB bz2, infobox-properties 922 MB bz2; Databus metadata licence CC BY 3.0). https://databus.dbpedia.org/dbpedia/mappings/mappingbased-literals
- **Country-level numeric tables are open, fresh and small enough. City level is not.** WDI is CC BY 4.0 with 1,500+ indicators, 200+ economies, 1960–2025, updated 2026-10-02; WDI_CSV.zip is 283 MB, which is > 100 MB and needs r4to's OK. https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/World-Development-Indicators. The Factbook ended on 2026-02-04, and factbook.json is CC0 (pushed 2026-09-11). https://github.com/factbook/factbook.json. No open city-level cost-of-living table was verified (the only CC BY claim is a vendor's).
- **Small models' comparison accuracy is a long-tail knowledge problem.** On ComparisonQA (DBpedia entity pairs), Phi-3.5-mini-4B scores 72.8 / 65.3 (high-/low-frequency entities) vs GPT-4o 93.9 / 85.8. https://arxiv.org/html/2412.20251v2 (Table 1)
- **Caution for injecting structured rows into a small reader.** In CRAG, adding KG results raises Llama-3-8B accuracy from 28.5 to 32.1, but hallucination goes 45.6 → 56.3 and truthfulness falls from −17.1 to −24.1. The 70B gains without that cost. https://arxiv.org/html/2406.04744v2 (Table 11)

Reading: when the facts reach the reader, the format/structure gap between 8B and frontier is ≈ 7 % (CompQA). When the reader must find and extract the attributes of every entity, the gap is ≈ 40 % (MEBench + RAG). Closed-book it is ≈ 75 %. BOAR's comparison ratio of 0.47 sits between the MEBench-RAG and closed-book regimes. That is consistent with "retrieval keeps no source for one or both sides" (SYNTHDEEP 1–2/7 both-sides-kept on the curated kit).

## Inferences
- The only comparison lever with a new mechanism is to **supply the side facts** in a compact, aligned form: a deterministic aligned card from FineWiki infoboxes, WDI/Factbook country rows and GeoNames city rows, placed in one slot. Better prompting (table-first, structured format) is not that lever. Prompt/format levers face a ≈ 7 % ceiling from the CompQA numbers, and they fall in the closed prompt families.
- The prior for the card is modest. Infobox/table sources add +1.8–3.4 pts on CompMix even at GPT-3.5 scale, and our packs already carry infobox text. CRAG shows structured injection can raise small-model hallucination. Expected Δ on V-para is likely below the MDE (0.03) unless the gate shows that comparison loss items concentrate on infobox/indicator facts.
- WDI and Factbook are the cleanest country tables (CC BY 4.0 / CC0, fresh or frozen-but-recent, small after slicing). City-level open comparison data is limited to GeoNames basics and EU-only Eurostat.
