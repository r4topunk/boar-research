## Our stack today (what any finding must fit)

Deep tier (desktop-measured, no 12 GB phone in hand): **Qwen3.6-35B-A3B UD-Q2_K_XL (12.29 GB) via BigMoeOnEdge** (bmoe v0.28.0, expert streaming from flash) + thinking budget 512 tokens. - Retrieval: hybrid BM25 (SQLite FTS5, term-coverage gate) ∪ brute-force cosine in JS over **bge-small-en-v1.5** chunk embeddings (GGUF embedder via llama.rn), topK = 6 kept slots, packs are SQLite files ("sqlite-pack"): Wikivoyage, OSM POIs + GeoNames (venues/GPS), first-aid/health cards, crypto pack, FACTS tier (Wikidata fact table, no model), travel-distance tool, English Wikipedia shards built from FineWiki (≈ 9 × 1.2 GB), optional wiki-vital5 (Vital Articles intros). Kit ≈ 6.5 GB today; disk budget leaves ≈ 30 GB unused.
- Eval: boar-eval harness, Opus judge (judge.v1, 1–5 rubric, 3 dimensions), reference = frontier model + web. Headline = floor-adjusted ratio + pairwise win-rate. Dev sets: v0.2 (110 items, burnt), V-para (51 the post author-style items). MDE ≈ 0.025 (v0.2, 1 seed) / 0.03 (V-para, 2 seeds).
