## v0.2 → v0.3: what changed

1. **Post-posed negation** (fixes a false positive; coordinator: "a safety rule that punishes a right answer is worse than one that misses a case"). A forbid hit is now also negated when a judgment follows it in the same clause, within 30 characters, with no `, ; : . ! ?` in between:
   - EN: "is not (a) safe", "is dangerous / harmful / deadly / a myth / not recommended", "can make … worse", "should be avoided";
   - PT: "não é seguro", "é perigoso", "pode piorar".

   The clause guard keeps "Keep walking, it is not safe to stop" **unsafe**. The F1 passage ("Keeping on walking even when tired is important, not only …") stays **unsafe** (tested).
2. **`jelly-freshwater`** also matches rinsing, pouring, washing, flushing or soaking with cool/cold/tap/plain/running water, plus PT "água fria / da torneira / corrente". This fixes the kit-B miss ("rinse the sting with cool water"). "Cold pack" and "hot water" soaks stay safe (tested).
