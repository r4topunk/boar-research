# Deep research 2026-10-12: published work and open source that can help BOAR reach the poidh #31 bar

Synthesis of `deepresearch-2026-10-12/` (CONTEXT, 9 angle notes, 5 gap notes, critique, 6 verification files; tally 5 confirmed, 18 corrected, 0 refuted). Where a `verify/*.md` file covers a number, the verified or corrected number is used and marked (Y). Numbers from notes without verification are marked (N). BOAR's own numbers are quoted as CONTEXT.md states them. The underlying notes are internal and not shipped; sections 3 (decisions, options, task lists) and 6 (open questions) of the internal version are also not published. No held-out item text was read for this report.

## TL;DR

- **Corrected premise 1 (WIKISECTION scope).** H5/H5b opened only `boar-wiki-en-top100k.sqlite` (98,389 articles, FineWiki Aug-2025) and gated only absent items with a kept source (14 of 34 on V-para). The 20 V-para no-kept absent items carry 0.333 of H's loss, 57 % of the absent bucket, and were never checked. The 15 shards (6,124,620 articles: top 1M in full, ≈ 5.1M as leads) and any fresh dump were never searched; 19.5 % of top-100k chunks (tables) are not FTS-indexed; 1/13 becomes 4/13 at p ≥ 0.6. "Facts live outside Wikipedia" is not established. `verify/wikisection_scope.md`
- **Corrected premise 2 (phone time).** Deep + think 512 on a Pixel 8 Pro CPU is ≈ 1.9–2.8 min per answer; the 3.5–5 min in CONTEXT is the 1024 budget. On Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 the released bmoe NPU-prefill path is ≈ 3.2 min (NPU saves ≈ 27 s of prefill, loses ≈ 24 s of decode) and needs the 22.3 GB Q4_K_M file. `verify/engine_speed.md`
- **Corrected premise 3 (freshness evidence).** A published project's +0.044 freshness gain (0.464 → 0.508) is one run per arm inside its own ±0.05 noise; the baseline already had a 2025-08 missing-articles pack; the rebuild also changed the renderer; out-of-corpus is n = 4; travel was flat (0.525 → 0.517); 0/63 wins in both arms. Expect less than +0.044 for BOAR. `verify/datastores.md`
- **Corrected premise 4 (bigger MoE).** Qwen3-Next-80B vs Qwen3.5-35B-think on IKP is +1.6 pp measured (0.564 vs 0.548), not the +5.2 pp the scaling law predicts; scaled by BOAR's 4B → 35B experience that is ≈ +0.01, below the MDE. `verify/generation_models.md`
- **Corrected premise 5 (other published percentages).** Percentages published by other offline-assistant projects are ratios of sums on 0–10 scores with no floor and are graded by different graders, so they are not comparable to judge.v1 floor-adjusted numbers. Read no gap into them. `verify/eval_judge.md`
- **Honest reading.** No published lever is shown to deliver +0.06 floor-adj on V-para, and the win rate is 0/51 everywhere. The deep tier needs a 12 GB-class phone and answers in 2–3 min.
- **What the bar means in loss terms.** V-para floor-adj 0.44 → 0.50 is +0.06 floor-adj ≈ +0.048 raw (floor-adj moves ≈ 1.25× raw between the 4B and deep+think runs), i.e. recovering about one-tenth of what the best configuration still loses. A one-sided significant ">0.50" on a 110-item v0.3 needs a point estimate of ≈ 0.53. `verify/eval_judge.md`, CONTEXT.md
