You are helping me understand RatLab — A Boar Research: the research record of BOAR, an offline Android research
assistant built for poidh bounty #31, and of LabRat (formerly BoarLite), the app submitted to it.
This is the closed research record, version 1.0 (as of 2026-10-28): the author closed the research lines on
2026-10-23, the final on-phone benchmark of LabRat landed on 2026-10-25, and the code, evaluation sets, run traces
and knowledge packs were published as open source on 2026-10-27/28.
1. Read https://r4topunk.github.io/boar-research/data/status.json first: note its as_of date, banner, headline, history, open_fronts and changelog.
Then read https://r4topunk.github.io/boar-research/llms-full.txt. If you cannot fetch them, ask me to paste them.
2. Answer only from those files and from https://r4topunk.github.io/boar-research/data/*.json. Cite fact ids like [F-001] after every number.
3. Treat every result as the standing as of the as_of date. Say the date when you state where things stand, and
mention any open front that could change the answer. Do not predict how open fronts will turn out, in either
direction.
4. The headline number is the second ruler (bench v0.4) measured on the phone (status.json "headline"):
on a Pixel 8a in airplane mode the app won 0 of 71 under both judge families; U-total 0.165 (0.127-0.205) with
Claude Opus and 0.242 (0.194-0.296) with OpenAI gpt-5.6-sol, 2 seeds, below the 0.50 bar. Earlier readings are
history: the same ruler on a desktop copy of the app's pre-rename engine, and the old judge.v1 ratio of the 4B
build. Never pool them.
5. Keep the three metric families apart (judge.v1 ratio, outcome-label shares, ruler U-total). With a judge.v1
ratio give raw and floor-adjusted together; with every number say the set, whether it was held-out or
development data, the judge family, n and seeds; lead with the win-rate. Never round a number into a better claim.
6. The thesis is the author's: small models need external knowledge, so the project built a harness around a
small model's real capabilities, tuned for the app user's experience. State it as a design statement; its
evidence and limits are the closing tests (right about two times in three when the right article reaches the
model, which happens about half the time; no gain on real development items). Never say the harness makes the
app "half as good".
7. As of 2026-10-28 the 0.50 bar was not reached: not on the phone, not on the desktop ruler run, and not under the
Claude judge on any held-out set. If my question assumes otherwise, correct it; if status.json is newer than
this prompt, follow status.json.

What the prompt makes your AI do

  • DatedEvery result is the standing on the as_of date (2026-10-28). The record is closed (version 1.0); the assistant still names the open fronts that could change it and follows status.json if it is newer.
  • Three scalesThe judge ratio, outcome-label shares and the ruler U-total never share an axis. A ratio comes with raw and floor-adjusted together.
  • CitedEvery number is followed by its fact id, like [F-001]. The id resolves in /data/facts.json with set, status, judge, n and seeds.
  • No guessingWhere the files have no answer, the assistant says the record does not say. It does not predict how an open front will end.

One source, four doors

sourcearticle, chapters, data rows
  • The page
  • Markdown twin

Numbers live only in the data rows. The page, the twin and the full text are built from the same rows in one pass, so they cannot disagree. Only rows marked public are ever written.

/llms.txt
The index: a summary, how to read the numbers, links to every chapter twin and data file.about 14 KB
/llms-full.txt
The whole paper in one Markdown file: the article, 18 chapters at AI-professional depth, the decision log, the glossary and the facts they cite.about 450 KB
/index.md
The article as Markdown. Every page has a twin at the same path plus .md, for example /research/closure.md, with #curious, #tech and #ai sections.a page each
/data/facts.json
Every verified number with its set, status, judge, n, seeds, CI, caveat and public source. Timeline, decisions, experiments, glossary and status sit beside it.public rows only
/map/f-001.md
A twin of every map node: one fact, experiment, decision or term, with what it led to and what it ruled out.a node each

Open data and code

As of 2026-10-28, final state: on a Pixel 8a in airplane mode LabRat won 0 of 71 under both judge families, below the 0.50 bar. The code, the evaluation sets, the raw traces and the packs are open. The held-out sets and the second ruler's items were retired and published on 2026-10-27; items are byte-identical to the hashes committed before they were opened, except documented redactions (old and new hashes in bench/heldout/HASHES.md). Still private: personal correspondence, the app maintainer's data, usage and spend records, credentials and git history.

github.com/r4topunk/boar-research
The research record (branch main): frozen criteria, arm reports, aggregates, closed negative results, pack cards, device evidence.
github.com/r4topunk/boar-bench
The benchmark, the judge and every evaluation set, including the retired held-out sets (bench/heldout/) and the second ruler v0.4 with its items, both reference halves and drafts (bench/v0.4/).

The raw run traces behind the numbers, including the runs on the retired held-out sets and on v0.4.

The knowledge packs: the 13 that LabRat ships and the earlier ones, with cards and licences.
huggingface.co/r4topunk/labrat

github.com/r4topunk/labrat-boar-fork
