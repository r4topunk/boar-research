#### Abstract

This is the record (v0.4-preprint, as of 2026-10-25; research lines closed 2026-10-23) of a five-week attempt to build an offline Android research assistant that is "more than 50 % as good as internet search plus a frontier AI", under a ≤ 12 GB RAM, ≤ 50 GB storage envelope (poidh bounty #31). Quality was first measured pairwise against frozen references written by Claude Opus with web search, judged by Claude Opus in both presentation orders on a 1–5 rubric, and reported as a raw ratio, a floor-adjusted ratio that removes the rubric minimum, and win/tie/loss; every deciding criterion was hashed before its run and held-out exposure was logged per touch. On a bounty-style set held out at run time (V-para, n = 51, two seeds) the frozen 4B build scores raw 0.413 (0.384–0.445), floor-adjusted 0.261 (0.224–0.300), and wins 0 of 51; a 35B mixture-of-experts with a 512-token thinking budget reaches floor-adjusted 0.436 (0.401–0.472) on development data, desktop only, 0 wins, and was not delivered. A cross-family judge raises all levels by ≈ 0.10 while preserving the ranking and a near-zero win-rate. Phone constraints then made a 1.7B model the main 8 GB engine (the 4B leads it by +0.109 under Opus on development data). A second ruler (75 items, half written by each judge family, frozen before any answer; U-total with a zero for non-answers; verdict = lower of two families) has been run on the desktop twin of the submitted app's pre-rename engine (one seed): 0 of 71 wins under both families and a lower-family U-total of 0.164 (0.123–0.206), below the 0.50 bar; we report it as history, because the headline is the same ruler run on the phone with the shipped app under a criterion frozen before generation, which is in progress. Our thesis is a design statement: small models need external knowledge, so we built a harness around a small model's real capabilities (health gate, code-computed arithmetic, pack search and delivery, a short answer style, a fixed check-the-source line, refusals). Its evidence and limits come from closing tests on pack-written self-tests: with the item's own article delivered, the 1.7B is right on 65 % of items vs 28 % otherwise (Claude Haiku outcome labels, one seed), delivery succeeds on 54 %, no pack reaches 0.75, and on real development items delivery does not separate answer quality. As of 2026-10-25, under the Claude judge, the 0.50 bar was not reached on the earlier held-out sets, and no unexposed held-out number exists. The submitted app, LabRat (formerly BoarLite), runs offline on an 8 GB phone; one search change in it was merged although its pre-registered safety criterion failed by the letter, under a trade-off its author accepted in advance, and we report both. We release the first benchmark and judge, a registry of negative results, openly licensed knowledge packs and a phone-parity study.

#### 1 Introduction: an underspecified target and a measurable one

The target phrase "more than 50 % as good as internet plus frontier AI" leaves the construct, the comparator, the question distribution and the judge unspecified. We operationalise it as a ratio of judged rubric scores between the system's answer and a frozen frontier-plus-web reference for the same question, on sets drawn to resemble the bounty's stated use (explanation, comparison, synthesis, reasoning). We make four contributions: (i) a public benchmark and pairwise judge with frozen references and a fail-closed pipeline; (ii) a pre-registered evaluation of a production-shaped offline system across seven frozen builds and a 35B deep tier; (iii) an analysis of the measuring instrument itself (rubric floor, seed versus judge noise, judge-family offset, reference length); (iv) a registry of negative results with power statements. We also document an unusual organisation: the work was coordinated and executed by AI agents under one human author, with independent AI reviewers in place of human sign-off.

#### Judge protocol

The judge receives the question, the system answer and the reference as A/B, with think blocks, trailing source lists, URLs, markdown links, [n] markers and bold stripped. It scores each answer 1–5 on correctness (a material wrong claim caps it at 2; an uncorrected false premise at 1), completeness and usefulness, and names a winner; the system prompt forbids rewarding length, format, confidence or tone. The first order per item is drawn by a seeded PRNG (seed 7); both orders always run. Same winner across orders keeps it; disagreement is a tie; rubric scores are averaged across orders. Position consistency is 96.5–100 % for Opus and Haiku.

*Facts: [F-166]*

#### 4 Discipline: frozen criteria, exposure ledger, fail-closed judging, and a judge funnel

Each experiment ("arm") compares one change with a named control under a criterion written before the deciding run and frozen by sha256; verdicts may add dated addenda but not edit the criterion. 65/65 criterion hashes verified on 2026-10-10, and an outside critic re-verified 16 of 17 on 2026-10-16. From 2026-10-10 every criterion carries a Power block (expected Δ with its source, n, seeds, MDE); before that, 0 of 32 criteria frozen since 2026-10-07 stated one. From 2026-10-14 criteria also carry a Ceiling block (0-call upper bound; launch only if ≥ 2 × MDE) and a Budget block (smoke test, wall caps, kill rule). Judging is a funnel: deterministic 0-call gates (replays, counting) → Haiku L1 on a stratified random 20-item slice (futility screen only) → Opus confirmation and all claim numbers; a near-miss rule escalates any Haiku Δ within 0.01 of a threshold to Opus. Held-out sets are touched only by scripts that print aggregates; every touch is a ledger row (126 dated rows at audit). Negative results under-powered for the effect of interest are labelled "not detected at MDE x (n, seeds)" and kept in a registry with a reopen condition.

## 3 · The floor trap {#floor-trap}

**Key facts:**
- Always-decline floor D = 0.306 raw (79 judged declines, 7 runs) [F-263].
- An always-decline app gets ≈ 61 % of the 0.50 bar for free [F-341].
- References average ≈ 4.9/5, so the ratio behaves like an absolute rubric mean [F-182].
- Worked example on the 4B: raw 0.512 = 0.387 floor-removed = 0.297 above always-decline [F-268].
- The floor-adjusted scale was already a **pre-registered companion metric** in the plan frozen on 10-02; only its promotion to headline came after results (group-A §2 item 6).

**Key decisions:** [D-011] (10-02: bar on the raw ratio, but always print raw + floor-removed side by side), [D-036] / [D-036] / [D-036] (10-06: headline = floor-adjusted + win/tie/loss), [D-065] (win-rate reported, never gated; history: the 10-08/09 arm reports did read the bar with a win-rate conjunct; "never gated" fixed in BAR-TABLE on 10-10).

**Turning point:** the 10-06 pipeline audit: "rigorous process, looping direction" (E-process timeline). The switch went toward the stricter scale; an outside critic later called it post-hoc but "not self-serving" [D-036].

#### Metrics

Let S_i and R_i be the order-averaged mean rubric of system and reference on item i. Raw = Σ S_i / Σ R_i. Floor-adjusted = Σ (S_i − 1) / Σ (R_i − 1). Above-decline = (raw − D)/(1 − D) with D = 0.306, the mean judged rubric of 79 declined answers over 7 runs divided by the mean reference rubric. Win/tie/loss is the order-combined winner per item. CIs: stratified (category) two-level bootstrap, items within stratum then runs within item, 2,000 resamples, seed 11, percentile; paired differences use the same procedure. For deep-tier rows we also report a common-mode CI that adds run-level seed variance: point ± 1.96·sqrt(SE_item² + (σ_run/√seeds)²).

#### 4 Discipline: frozen criteria, exposure ledger, fail-closed judging, and a judge funnel

Each experiment ("arm") compares one change with a named control under a criterion written before the deciding run and frozen by sha256; verdicts may add dated addenda but not edit the criterion. 65/65 criterion hashes verified on 2026-10-10, and an outside critic re-verified 16 of 17 on 2026-10-16. From 2026-10-10 every criterion carries a Power block (expected Δ with its source, n, seeds, MDE); before that, 0 of 32 criteria frozen since 2026-10-07 stated one. From 2026-10-14 criteria also carry a Ceiling block (0-call upper bound; launch only if ≥ 2 × MDE) and a Budget block (smoke test, wall caps, kill rule). Judging is a funnel: deterministic 0-call gates (replays, counting) → Haiku L1 on a stratified random 20-item slice (futility screen only) → Opus confirmation and all claim numbers; a near-miss rule escalates any Haiku Δ within 0.01 of a threshold to Opus. Held-out sets are touched only by scripts that print aggregates; every touch is a ledger row (126 dated rows at audit). Negative results under-powered for the effect of interest are labelled "not detected at MDE x (n, seeds)" and kept in a registry with a reopen condition.
