Reading it

  • Every fact has a stable id (the letter F and a number). It resolves in facts.json. Fact rows carry set, set status, judge, n, seeds, CI and caveat; a missing field is null, never guessed.
  • Three metric families never share an axis: the judge ratio (raw and floor-adjusted), outcome-label shares, and the ruler’s U-total.
  • Where a row was superseded, the old row stays and points to the newer one.
  • The record is a living preprint. status.json says as_of, what is still open and what changed.
